Cheap by design

A flat fee for the platform, and you pay for the AI only when you use it. Everyday requests go to a low-cost model. For the rare question that needs more, use your own key for a frontier model.

Privacy

Not a seat on someone’s platform. Your own instance.

Every household gets its own Maurice, with its own server, databases and documents. Nothing in it is pooled with other customers, and nothing is used to train anyone’s model.

Inside the house, each member’s conversations are sealed too. Being the admin doesn’t let you read anyone else’s.
