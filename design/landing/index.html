What we believe

We believe AI can be used for something other than holding on to us. That it can help each of us reread our own life, understand what is on our mind, and say it in our own words. That what we share is better when it comes from within. And that none of this requires handing our data to anyone.

Your host

Ten years of mail, three years of conversations. He has already read it all.

Maurice is the host, and a good host knows his regulars. Connect your mailbox, drop in your ChatGPT or Claude history: he draws out the subjects that matter in your life, the people, what is under way. You have nothing to explain to him.

He asks before reading your mail, and he has no way to send or delete a message.

Your table

A table for a few people close to you, where the host waits to be called.

Your circle is the people you sit down with: a partner, a spouse, the members of a project you run together. You trade ideas in shared conversations, around a context you hold in common.

Maurice is there, but he stays quiet until someone turns to him. And what he knows about each of you in private stays away from the table.

The house

Not a seat on someone’s platform. Your own instance.

Every circle gets its own Maurice, with its own server, databases and documents. Nothing in it is pooled with other customers, and nothing is used to train anyone’s model.

Inside the circle, each member’s conversations are sealed too. Being the admin doesn’t let you read anyone else’s.
