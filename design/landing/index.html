What we believe

We believe AI can be used for something other than holding on to us. That it can help each of us reread our own life, understand what is on our mind, and say it in our own words. That what we share is better when it comes from within. And that none of this requires handing our data to anyone.

Candide Kemmler, Brussels

Introspection

Time spent with yourself.

Your past conversations with ChatGPT or Claude, your mail, what you highlight as you read: Maurice gathers them into a garden of notes that is yours alone. You come back to it, reread it, add to it.

The AI

It digests. You decide.

Maurice sorts, summarises and connects. What he writes points to where it came from, and what he worked out on his own stays marked as his until you’ve confirmed it.

Creation

Original, and documented.

What you write rests on what you have actually read and lived: the passage, the date and the source are there when you need them. Maurice notices what is on your mind – he can even whisper that it’s time to write it down.

Expression · in the works

Publish because you have something to say.

A site of your own: the books and films that stayed with you, your notes, a blog. In your voice, with no algorithm in between.

Company

Invite people in. Follow no one.

A household is a place you open to whoever you choose: family, a friend passing through, a guest for one conversation. No followers, no audience to feed.

Your data

For you, not about you.

Your household has its own space on a server in the EU. No ads, nothing used to train anyone’s model, and you can leave with everything.

The household

A circle you trust, where the AI waits to be asked.

A household is a few people close to you: family, friends, those you trust. You trade ideas in shared conversations, around a context you hold in common.

Maurice is there, but he stays quiet until someone turns to him. And what he knows about each of you in private stays out of the room.

Privacy

Not a seat on someone’s platform. Your own instance.

Every household gets its own Maurice, with its own server, databases and documents. Nothing in it is pooled with other customers, and nothing is used to train anyone’s model.

Inside the house, each member’s conversations are sealed too. Being the admin doesn’t let you read anyone else’s.
