Support

Help with the Maurice and Carnet apps

Write to us at support@chezmaurice.eu. Say which app and device you use, and the address of your household if you have one. We answer within two working days.

The apps

  • Maurice (iPhone, iPad, Mac) is where you talk with Maurice, alone or in rooms with the members of your household.
  • Carnet (iPhone, iPad) is where you keep and read: books, articles, films, podcasts, and the notes you write on them.

Both connect to the server of your household — one you run yourself, or one we host for you at <name>.chezmaurice.eu.

Joining a household

Enter the household’s address, then the invite code its administrator gave you, and choose a PIN. The same code works in both apps. Lost your code? Ask your household’s administrator for a new one in Settings → Members. To get a household of your own, see chezmaurice.eu; to run one yourself, see the self-hosting guide.

Your account and your data

  • Export or erase: in Maurice, Settings → My data.
  • Delete your account: in Maurice, Settings → My data → Delete my account; in Carnet, tap your badge, then Privacy → Delete my account.
  • How your data is handled: the privacy policy.

Safety

In a shared room, long-press a message to report it or to block its author. To report the app or a server being used to cause harm, write to safety@chezmaurice.eu. The rules are in the terms of use.

Something is not working

  • “Cannot reach the server”: check the household’s address and your connection; a household you run must be reachable from where you are.
  • A wrong PIN too many times: wait a quarter of an hour and try again.
  • No notifications: allow them for the app in iOS Settings.

Anything else: support@chezmaurice.eu.
