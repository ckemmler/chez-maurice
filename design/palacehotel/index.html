Palacehotel Software SRL

A small Belgian software company. It designs, builds, hosts and maintains software — and publishes Chez Maurice.

The company

Palacehotel Software was incorporated in Brussels on 20 December 2007 and has been building software ever since: design and development, database and web work, technical consultancy, and the operation of the infrastructure that runs it.

It is run by Candide Kemmler, its founder and sole manager. It is small on purpose — the person who discusses the work is the person who does it.

What it does

  • Design and development of software, on the web and on Apple platforms
  • Hosting, data processing and the operation of computer infrastructure
  • Technical consultancy and architecture
  • Publication and operation of its own products

Products

Chez Maurice

An open-source household AI: one shared assistant and memory for a family or a small group, running on a machine its owners control, with a private wiki for each person and native apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
