# Maurice — the digest

A condensed reference to the system documentation ([[maurice-docs]] and the notes under it), rewritten 19 September 2026 (late evening, after the sessions that gave the domains their kind and let Maurice open a conversation). Each section states what a part *is*, the names a person needs (routes, files, scripts, variables), what ships versus what only exists, and the known gaps. The history of how each thing came to be is in the full notes; this keeps the facts. The `covers` map in the frontmatter names the note dates this digest reflects: a note updated since is read in full beside it.

Two distinctions run through everything. **Ships vs. exists**: the public product is chat + the garden + corpus search; the wider capabilities (Calibre, tasks, coaching, health, tracks, mail…) live in the private `maurice-tools` overlay, are in daily use at home, and are *experimental*. **Vision vs. built**: the founding vision ([[maurice]]) describes a household AI with a tamed, visible memory; these docs describe what is built and flag the gaps.

## Concept — the why

Maurice is a self-hosted **household AI assistant** with a scoped, visible memory: the *garden*, a folder of Markdown notes per member, git-backed, that grows only by deliberate human action — nothing enters it without an explicit request. "Apprivoisé" (tamed) is the stance: the member chooses exactly what Maurice knows for a conversation. There is one Maurice; the *domains* of a member's life are what he follows closely, each with a brief he keeps. Sovereignty means the data stays where the household put it: no cloud account, no analytics, no email; and since September 2026 a single European supplier, Scaleway, can provide both the machine and the model, so nothing need leave the EU. "Small on purpose": the right-sized win is a few hundred households, used by people who are not its author.

Vision items **not built**: the "temporal mirror" (automatic daily/weekly reviews), Maurice noticing patterns and proposing notes (not intended either), emergent hats derived from the active note subtree (closed by another route since 19 September 2026: a domain *is* the scope, proposed from the conversations rather than derived from a folder). The **domains** design (the owner's French note *maurice-domaines*: domains that emerge from the conversations, each with a visible brief, proposed by Maurice, the garden seeded on consent) is being built session by session — see *Domains and their briefs*.

## Architecture — the five parts

- **Maurice app** (`app/Maurice`, SwiftUI, one target for iPhone/iPad/Mac): the conversation client. Holds no authoritative state; pairs to a server, authenticates a member, talks HTTPS + WebSocket. Dictation, cost meter, composer; since 19 September 2026 one Maurice and, beside the conversations, the domains list with their briefs, where it used to offer a roster of specialized Maurices.
- **Carnet** (separate repo `carnet/`, iOS/iPadOS): the pocket companion for capturing and reading — log signals, read books and articles, browse the garden, save what you read via a share sheet.
- **Server** (`server/`, one Bun process on Hono): `server/src` is the chat engine (auth, users, conversations, domains and reading companions, files, gardens, composer, models, reports, the streaming agentic loop, the nightly jobs); `server/data-api` is the personal-data layer at `/api/v1/*` (health, tasks, signals, tracks, coaching, Calibre, bank, layouts, places, uploads, and the garden's media: articles, entries, résonances, flashcards).
- **Tools** (`tools/`): Python MCP servers behind one `mcp_gateway`, reached by the server over loopback. Public: `garden`, `corpus`, `shared`, `mcp_gateway`. Private overlay (`maurice-tools` repo, symlinked): tracks, health, signals, tasks, calendar, contacts, calibre, readwise, compte, social, layouts, thoughts, mail, pipelines.
- **Web** (`web/`, Astro): renders each member's garden as a themeable site under `/g/<member>`, and the public site (candide.me) on Cloudflare Pages.
- Plus a **browser clipper** (`clients/web-clipper`) that saves the rendered DOM of a page as an article fiche, like Carnet's share sheet.

**A message's path.** `POST /api/conversations/:id/messages` → stored and fanned to the room over WebSocket (in a multi-human room Maurice replies only when summoned with `@maurice`/`@claude`) → system prompt (household, profile, the bound domain or companion, composer context, domain briefs in a private conversation, tool roster, safety floor last) → history bounded to the model's window → tools filtered by families → streamed request with cache breakpoints → NDJSON `StreamEvent`s (`text_delta`, `thinking`, `ping`, `tool_call`, `tool_data`, `usage`, `done`, `error`) → tools fed back, up to 6 rounds → the app renders prose live and data cards from the data itself. The turn belongs to the conversation, not the request (`GET …/turn` re-attaches).

**Delivery: a container, and only a container** (decided 14 September 2026; the notarized `.pkg` is retired). `infra/container/Dockerfile` has two targets: `dev` (bind-mounted sources, Calibre, the private overlays) and `production` (sources copied in, 2 MCP tools — garden, corpus — no Calibre, ~2 GB, behind Caddy with Let's Encrypt; the build fails on a dangling symlink rather than ship a private repo). Base image Ubuntu 26.04 (arm64). `scripts/deploy.sh <host> [tag]` builds on the Mac, pushes through a registry (`MAURICE_REGISTRY`) or pipes over ssh, and recreates the households; `infra/cloud-init/maurice.yaml` prepares a machine; `infra/container/MULTI-HOUSEHOLD.md` + `ops/household.sh` run several households on one machine as compose projects behind one Caddy, each on a loopback admin port from 3101 up. `scripts/container.sh` drives the local container (`seed` snapshots the live SQLite databases with `VACUUM INTO`; `import <archive>` starts one from a household archive). No cron in the container: the nightly jobs live in the server process.

**A household costs ~260 MB idle** (measured 14 September 2026), so an 8 GB machine carries about twenty. **The home Mac is the exception**: it still runs launchd agents (`scripts/service.sh`): `com.maurice.api` (port 3001), `com.maurice.mcp-gateway`, `com.maurice.web`, nightly `com.maurice.backup`, and one `com.maurice.household.<name>` per extra household; it reads `~/.maurice/config.toml` and `~/.maurice/.env`.

**The first rented host, `maurice-fleet`** (since 17 September 2026): a Scaleway BASIC2-A2C-8G in Paris (2 ARM vCPU, 8 GB, Ubuntu 26.04, 51.15.217.40) carrying Aline's household and the App Review one; `MAURICE_REGISTRY=rg.fr-par.scw.cloud/maurice scripts/deploy.sh maurice-fleet` is the whole update (an already-built tag is the rollback). Everything Scaleway sits in a project `maurice` with one IAM key limited to Generative APIs and the registry (`~/.maurice/ops/scaleway-fleet.env`). Backed up nightly (see *Backups*). The demo fleet (throwaway instances, TTL a couple of weeks, public-domain seed) is the planned sibling — the answer to the cold-start problem.

**Networking** is the operator's plumbing, not Maurice's: Tailscale (private mesh, at home) or a Cloudflare Tunnel (public hostname). Trap: `brew services restart cloudflared` drops `tunnel run` from the plist.

**Gaps**: git is assumed present on the host (the server shells out to system `git`); the vector store lives in the repo tree (`tools/corpus/data`) rather than the data dir; no automatic provider failover. Hosted volumes unbacked was a gap until 19 September 2026 and is closed (restic, nightly).

## The server

`server/index.ts` mounts both halves. Listens on `PORT`, serves generated images and avatars, proxies `/mcp/*` to the gateway and `/g/<member>/` to the garden engine (both hardcoded to `127.0.0.1`), upgrades WebSockets for rooms (`roomBus.ts`: a per-room channel and a per-user channel at `/api/me/ws`).

**Chat-engine routes** (`server/src/routes`): `/api/auth` (setup, login, enroll, invite/pairing codes) · `/api/users` (roster, profiles, avatars, preferences, API tokens, guest contacts) · `/api/conversations` (threads and rooms, messages, streaming replies, `GET /:id/turn`, `POST /:id/turn/stop`, participants, read state, tool-family overrides, `/search`) · `/api/maurices` (the rows of `maurices` — domains and reading companions, `kind` on `POST`/`PATCH`; headed by Maurice Maurice) · `/api/domains` (`GET /` lists the member's domains and companions; `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /:id/brief`, `POST /:id/brief/refresh`; creator only, 404 for a companion) · `/api/admin/conversations/open` (a conversation Maurice opens, operator only) · `/api/files` · `/api/v1/gardens` · `/api/v1/composer` · `/api/models` · `/api/tool-families` · `/api/reports` · `/api/ancillary` (loopback-only model turns for the Python tools) · `/api/me/usage`, `/api/admin/usage` (spend against the tightest cap; the admin's view lists the night last, "Maurice, at night") · `/api/admin/export` (the household archive, streamed, admin token; `GET /admin/export` behind the console cookie) · `/api/admin/status` (docs-refresh state) · `/admin`, `/login` (the admin web UI: members, models, provider keys, Calibre, access grid, caps, ancillary pins, the corpus, briefs and docs cards — `POST /admin/corpus/reconcile`, `/admin/briefs/run`, `/admin/docs/refresh` — the export). **Data-api routes** under `/api/v1/*`: `health/*`, `signals`, `coaching`, `tasks`, `tracks`, `dossiers`, `layouts`, `dashboard`, `places`, `uploads`, `compte`, `bank-transactions`; `garden-tools/*` (the owner's toolbar); `calibre/*`, `books`; `garden/articles`, `garden/entries`, `garden/links`, `garden/cards`; `articles/scrape` (legacy alias).

**The turn registry** (`services/turns.ts`, since 19 September 2026). A turn streams NDJSON `StreamEvent`s (`thinking` throttled to one a second, text never forwarded; `ping` after 15 s of silence; `usage` once before `done`). The reply in flight is kept in memory per conversation, so a client that vanishes no longer stops it: the reply runs to the end, is persisted and fanned out over the room socket. `GET /api/conversations/:id/turn` re-attaches: `204` when nothing is known, else a `resume` line (`text`, `data`, `tool`, `usage`, `started_at`, `finished`) then the live events; a finished turn stays reachable 60 s. `POST …/turn/stop` aborts and keeps what streamed. One reply at a time per conversation: a second summons is `409` (the human message is still stored).

**The agentic loop** (`streamResponse` in `services/claude.ts`): system prompt (household, profile, the bound domain or companion, composer context, then — private conversation only, never in a room, not for Maurice Maurice — the member's domain briefs under 3 000 tokens, then a *Your tools* section naming exactly the families present, the safety floor last) → messages (the clock as a trailing system reminder, never in the system prompt, so the prefix stays cacheable; an assistant turn that called tools carries a *tool trail* — six results at most, 240 characters each) → model and provider → MCP tools sorted by name → **context-window fit** (`contextWindow.ts`: the model's `ctx` less the head, a reply reserve and a margin; over that the oldest turns are dropped to 70% of budget with hysteresis, the cut stored as `context_from`, the model told the beginning is missing) → stream; tool-use blocks run (web search via Tavily, or an MCP `callTool`), results minified and fed back; Stop reaches the provider on every path and is checked between tool calls; an OpenAI-style round silent for 180 s is abandoned; **`MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 6`**. In a multi-human room Maurice answers only when mentioned (`mentionsMaurice()`); ➤ posts `{ summon: true, maurice_id }`, 💬 a human-only message.

**Prompt caching.** Anthropic: three explicit breakpoints (last system block, end of history, the tool-result trail), a cached read at a tenth. Z.ai and Scaleway cache implicitly; Mistral and OpenAI only with a `prompt_cache_key` (the conversation id; `PROMPT_CACHE_KEY_PROVIDERS`). On Scaleway only DeepSeek V4 Flash reports cached tokens (priced at 0.2).

**Providers** (chosen per resolved model's `models.provider`, no failover): **Anthropic** (native loop, key `households.api_key`); **Ollama** (local, `ollama.ts`, a 32k window requested); **OpenAI / Mistral / Z.ai / Scaleway** through one Chat-Completions door, `openaiChat.ts` (`openaiStyleBaseUrl()` in `claude.ts`; a project-scoped Scaleway key needs `api.scaleway.ai/<project>/v1`, hence `scaleway_project_id`). Reasoning models stream thinking under `reasoning_content` or `reasoning`. **A key is what makes a model exist**: `configuredProviders()` gates the apps' roster, the admin grid (callable models only; hidden rows carried over on save) and the ancillary pins. Seeded rosters: Anthropic (Claude Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5), OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini), Mistral (Large, Small), Z.ai (GLM-5.3 text-only, GLM-5.3 Flash with `vision`, 1M windows), Scaleway (ten Serverless models: Mistral Small 3.2 — the Scaleway everyday default, reads images — Gemma 4 26B, Qwen 3.6 35B, GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek V4 Flash, Qwen 3.5 397B, Qwen 3 235B, Llama 3.3 70B, Mistral Medium 3.5, GLM 5.2; output capped at 16k/32k; a 429 "INSUFFICIENT QUOTA" is a per-minute rate limit). `pricing.ts` prices every seeded model (Scaleway in euros at a fixed ECB rate, `EUR_USD`); an unpriced model prices at `null`, never zero.

**The reasoning switch** (since 18 September 2026): `models.thinking` ∈ `none` / `optional` / `always` says whether a model reasons and whether that can be switched; a persona records its choice (`maurices.thinking`, null / on / off), the household one for the everyday Maurice (`households.everyday_thinking`, seeded to "answer directly", admin console only); each provider path translates it — Z.ai `thinking: {type: enabled|disabled}`, Anthropic 4.6+ `thinking: {type: adaptive|disabled}` (thinking blocks replayed with their signature in tool rounds), Ollama `think: true|false`. Scaleway's reasoning models are `always` and ignore it. Nothing is sent when the persona made no choice or the model has no switch.

**Ancillary models** (`services/ancillary.ts`, since 13 September 2026): everything that calls a model and is not the chat — summaries, flashcards, signal parsing, the tools' classifiers, the night's briefs — is an *invocation* resolved pin (`ancillary_models`) → household `ancillary_model` → chat default, skipping models whose provider has no key. Each tier (light/standard/heavy) has a European preferred list: Scaleway first (Mistral Small 3.2, GPT-OSS 120B, Qwen 3.5 397B), then Mistral; Anthropic and OpenAI out by decision, Z.ai by size. `domain_brief` and `domain_mapping` carry their own `prefer` list (19 September 2026): DeepSeek V4 Flash, then Mistral Small 3.2 on Scaleway, then Mistral's own Small. Pins record `source` (`auto` follows the advice; `admin` never moves); `ancillary_advised` remembers which invocations were ever offered advice; a deleted pin is never re-created; only invocations whose tool directory is present are offered. The Python tools call `POST /api/ancillary` (loopback only, `middleware/loopback.ts`) through `tools/shared/model_config.complete()`; a turn reports its usage (`AncillaryResult.usage`, `complete_full()`), and a request may name an explicit `model` for an experiment. The research-pipeline invocations are `ownDispatch`. The admin card sits under *Advanced*, collapsed.

**The spending fuse** (`budget.ts`): a no-op unless a cap is set; it *refuses* where `pricing.ts` *measures*. **Three layers, the tightest wins**: the instance's env caps (`MAURICE_SPEND_CAP_USD` lifetime, `MAURICE_SPEND_CAP_DAILY_USD` rolling 24 h; 10 USD daily on the fleet host) ≥ the household's daily cap (`households.spend_cap_daily_usd`, on the console) ≥ the member's (`users.spend_cap_daily_usd`, on the member's edit page). Checked at the top of every agentic round with the turn's running cost passed in; refuses an unpriced model under any cap; Ollama exempt by provider; the refusal names the closest cap and emits usage then an `error` event. Spend is written to `spend_ledger` by `addMessage` with `user_id` (in a room, the sender of the message Maurice answered). **A fourth spender, the night** (19 September 2026): the domain briefs' calls carry `user_id = 'system'` under their own allowance `households.spend_cap_system_daily_usd` ("Night's daily allowance"), weighed where a member's cap would be (neither touches the other; household and instance caps cover both), checked *before* the call; the real cost (reasoning tokens included) is recorded whether the brief was kept or not. `GET /api/me/usage` returns `today_usd` (24 rolling hours), `month_usd` (calendar month), `cap_daily_usd` (the tightest, or null), `remaining_usd`; `GET /api/admin/usage` returns everyone's plus the night.

**Auth**: admin created at `POST /api/auth/setup`, logs in with username + password; standard members with `user_id` + a mandatory PIN (rate-limited per IP); guests enroll without one; `POST /api/auth/enroll` redeems an invite code (rate-limited per IP and globally); pairing tokens are the one-shot programmatic path. Opaque session tokens; `maur_*` API tokens scoped `mcp` / `health` / `full` (`health` opens only `/healthz`'s full face; `mcp` is not enforced). Roles `admin` / `standard` / `guest`. **The admin dashboard is loopback-only** on three signals: loopback socket peer (Bun's `requestIP`), loopback `Host`, no Cloudflare edge header — a hosted household's `/admin` is a permanent 403, reached through an ssh forward (`ops/admin.ts <name>`).

**Health & ops**: `GET /healthz` public (`status`, `service`, `version`) or, with a token, `git_sha`, `built_at`, `schema_version`, `uptime_s`, `db` (a `quick_check` cached five minutes, `DB_CHECK_TTL_MS`), `disk_free_mb`, `errors_1h` / `errors_24h` / `last_error_at` / `last_error_kind`; 503 when degraded. Version from `git describe` first, then `server/build-info.json` (`scripts/build-info.sh`); `MAURICE_VERSION` / `MAURICE_GIT_SHA` / `MAURICE_BUILT_AT` override (`services/buildInfo.ts`). Operator tooling in `ops/`: `fleet.yaml` (inventory), `fleet-status.ts` (one table, exit 1 when anything is down), `tower.ts` (TUI: `d` deploys, `R` restarts, `a` opens an admin console), `admin.ts`, `recreate-container.sh`, `mint-health-token.ts`, `backup.sh`; no discovery, no central registry, no fleet UI.

**Documentation refresh** (`services/mauriceDocsRefresh.ts`, since 19 September 2026): `MAURICE_DOCS_URL` names where Maurice Maurice's notes are published (default the repo's `docs/maurice/` on `main`; `off` disables). 20 s after boot, then every 24 h, the server GETs `manifest.json` (15 s timeout, 64 KB cap, the SSRF guard; `MAURICE_DOCS_ALLOW_LOCAL=1` lifts it); if `generated_at` is newer, it downloads each note whose sha256 differs (2 MB cap, hash verified), stages the set and swaps it into `~/.maurice/docs/maurice`; any failure leaves the previous set. `docsDir()` chooses `MAURICE_DOCS_DIR` if set, else the refreshed set when at least as new as the bundle, else the bundle. `GET /api/admin/status` carries `docs: { source, generated_at, last_check_at, last_error }`.

**The corpus reconciles itself nightly** (`services/corpusNightly.ts`, since 19 September 2026): from 03:00 local, once per calendar day (a ten-minute tick, so a server down at 03:00 catches up), the server asks the corpus to reconcile every conversation (`corpus__index_conversation` with no id — a hash diff per message) as a background task, polls `corpus__reconcile_status` every fifteen seconds (three hours at most), then prunes per member the file-backed entries whose file is gone and, on the first member's call, the shared pool (`corpus__prune`, `shared: true`). Last run in `<app dir>/corpus-nightly.json`; `MAURICE_CORPUS_NIGHTLY=off` disables.

**The domain briefs are rewritten nightly** (`services/domainBriefs.ts`, since 19 September 2026): at 04:00 local, an hour after the corpus, same shape (`<app dir>/domain-briefs-nightly.json`, `MAURICE_DOMAIN_BRIEFS=off`). Mechanics in *Domains and their briefs*.

**Backups.** *Hosted households* (since 19 September 2026): `infra/host/backup.sh` runs from the host's crontab at 03:30 UTC — the container writes a consistent copy of every SQLite database (`VACUUM INTO`), then restic (its own container, the volume read-only) sends the volume minus the live databases to one repository per host on Scaleway Object Storage (bucket `maurice-fleet-backups`, project `maurice`), 14 daily / 8 weekly / 12 monthly kept, encrypted with a password only the Mac holds (`~/.maurice/ops/fleet-backup.env`). `ops/backup.sh restore-test <host> <name>` rehearses a restore into a throwaway household; `ops/backup.sh restore` does it for real. *The Mac*: `com.maurice.backup` snapshots `maurice.db` and `life.db` nightly into `~/.maurice/backups/db` (`scripts/backup-db.sh`); gardens rely on git; `compte.db`, `recommendations.db` and the git-ignored flashcards are not covered.

**Gaps**: above the fuse nothing yet — no balance, no prepaid quota, no statement a customer can read; the ancillary calls made *for a member* (summaries, flashcards, signal parses) have no spender and the ledger never sees them; Scaleway's cache invisible on nine models out of ten; the turn registry is memory only (a restart forgets every reply in flight); the tool trail is plain text in history; no failover; a moved household keeps stale local models; context estimated by characters; the Mac's backup is partial; `mcp` scope unenforced; an error collector undecided; the tracks data-api routes carry no member check.

## The data model

One SQLite file, `maurice.db` (WAL), in `~/.maurice/`; the whole schema is `server/src/db.ts` — `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` plus append-only guarded `ALTER`s and idempotent backfills, no migration framework, `PRAGMA user_version` stamped by `SCHEMA_VERSION`. The data-api keeps its own files under `~/.maurice/data/` (`MAURICE_DATA_DIR`): `life.db` (highlights, reading positions, health, tasks, signals, dossiers, coaching, layouts, places), `compte.db`, `recommendations.db`; plus the corpus vectors and the Calibre library.

- **Identity**: `households` (one row `id='default'`: the provider keys `api_key`, `openai_api_key`, `mistral_api_key`, `zai_api_key`, `scaleway_api_key` + `scaleway_project_id`, `fal_api_key`; `default_model`, `ancillary_model`, `max_tokens`, `everyday_thinking` (nullable 0/1, seeded 0), `spend_cap_daily_usd`, `spend_cap_system_daily_usd` (the night's allowance), `ollama_host`, `default_tool_families`, `color`, `icon`, the `*_seeded` guards); `users` (`username` is also the garden directory; `role`, `pin_hash`, `profile_text`, `experimental_tools`, `everyday_model`, `spend_cap_daily_usd`); `user_preferences` (theme, font, density, palette, locale).
- **Auth & devices**: `sessions`, `api_tokens` (`token_hash`, `scope`), `invite_codes`, `device_tokens` (APNs, with `household_tag`), `devices` (pairing, unused by the app), `guest_contacts`.
- **Conversations**: `conversations` (`maurice_id` null = everyday, `origin` `'anthropic'` for imports, `tool_families`, `context_from`, `opened_by` ∈ `member`/`maurice` — `maurice` reserved for the conversation the night will open to propose domains; nothing writes it yet); `messages` (`author_id`, `maurice_id`, `model`, `data` = frozen tool results for the data cards, `usage`); `messages_fts` (FTS5, trigger-maintained); `conversation_summaries` (keyed on a transcript hash); `conversation_participants` (`last_read_at`); `reports`; `spend_ledger` (`at`, `provider`, `model`, `cost_usd`, `user_id` indexed with `at`; pre-column rows stay null and count only in household sums; `user_id = 'system'` is the night — no row in `users`; a deleted message never un-spends).
- **Files**: `folders`, `files` (bytes at `{dataDir}/files/{id}{ext}`).
- **Gardens**: no `gardens` table — `note_shares` (owner, slug, member) and `garden_settings` (audience key → `web_theme`); the content is Markdown under `~/.maurice/gardens/<username>/`, one git repo per member.
- **Domains & composer**: `maurices` (`kind` = `domain` — the default, NULL reads as such — or `companion`, sorted once at first start on 19 September 2026; `hat` and `palette` are the personas' leftovers, written and read by nothing; `thinking` nullable 0/1); `conversations.opened_by` (`maurice` when he opened it); `users.is_child`; `households.maurice_opens_min_days`; `domain_briefs` (`maurice_id` + `member_id` PK, both cascade; `text`, `updated_at`, `sources_json` = the conversations the last rewrite read, `read_until` = the newest message it saw, `model` = what wrote it, `member` when the member corrected it); `maurice_access`; `composer_specs` (keyed conversation + account); both `context_json` and `spec_json` are frozen resolved sets.
- **Models**: `models` (`tier`, `vendor`, `provider`, `ctx` in k tokens, `vision`, `thinking` ∈ `none`/`optional`/`always` — Ollama fills it from `/api/show`; prices are in `pricing.ts`, not here), `user_model_access` (standard members' allow-list; admins computed-all), `ancillary_models` (`invocation`, `model_id`, `source`), `ancillary_advised` (`invocation`, `at`).
- **Bridge**: `calibre_libraries` (one global library, set in the admin).

Tool families resolve **conversation → bound row → household default → tier default**. **Gaps**: single household per server; room context shared not per-participant; the ledger counts money not tokens (an unpriced model records nothing) and the ancillary calls made on a member's behalf are charged to nobody; hand-rolled migrations (the guest-role rebuild of `users` once dropped `everyday_model` and `experimental_tools`, fixed 19 September 2026 with a test); test isolation by environment (lazy imports).

## The MCP tool ecosystem

`tools/mcp_gateway/server.py` discovers every tool server under `tools/` at startup (three shapes: `gateway_context`, a module-level `app`, or a FastMCP `mcp`), namespaces tools as `{server}__{tool}` (`garden__create_note`, `signals__signal_log`), serves a unified endpoint and per-server ones over Streamable HTTP. Per-member context comes from the `X-Maurice-Member-Id` header into a contextvar (`tools/shared/context.py`: `get_member_id()` / `require_member_id()`). Auth: a bearer token (`MAURICE_MCP_TOKEN`) for the loopback caller, and OAuth 2.1 / PKCE (`oauth.py`) accepting `maur_*` tokens.

The engine's client is `server/src/services/mcpClient.ts`, through the `/mcp/*` proxy. **Tool families** gate the roster: the tier default is empty for every model; only `web` and `signals` are always on; `garden` is sub-split into `garden-notes`, `garden-journal`, `garden-people`, `garden-fragments`, `garden-media`, `garden-publish`, `garden-other` (`services/toolFamilies.ts`). Everything but `web`, `signals`, `garden-notes`, `garden-journal` is **experimental** and hidden until an admin ticks *experimental tools* on the member. The system prompt names only the families actually present (`toolRosterNotice()`), web search only when Tavily is configured, image generation only when a fal key exists.

**Public vs. private** is enforced by `.gitignore` and the container's dockerignore: public `garden`, `corpus`, `shared`, `mcp_gateway`; everything else is the `maurice-tools` overlay. Secrets in `.env`, never in tracked config.

**`mail`** (private overlay, September 2026): reads Proton (via Bridge) and iCloud mailboxes; the only write is `triage(account, uid, action)` over a closed vocabulary in the config (states `01 · Répondre`… `Archive`, topic labels; one state and at most one topic per message); no send, no delete, no arbitrary move; bodies returned stripped and wrapped as untrusted; deterministic `[rules]` run before the model (46% of the inbox); a nightly launchd proposal (`com.maurice.mail-proposal`) classified by local `qwen3.6:35b-a3b` with reasoning off; an audit log at `~/.maurice/mail-audit.jsonl` keyed on Message-ID; reading horizons per state (120 days, 180 for `to_reply`). Its config is one person's method and gates sharing the code.

**Gaps**: the gateway itself enforces neither families nor the experimental flag (a direct MCP client gets the full roster); `web` and `signals` cannot be turned off; the parent `garden` prefix opens all sub-families; no per-tool sandboxing; discovery is positional; résonances and flashcards have no MCP face; Homebrew's `python@3.14` needs `scripts/fix-python-expat.sh` after every upgrade; `tools/calendar` shadows the stdlib module.

## The web garden

`web/` is the Astro engine. **Themes** live under `web/themes/<name>/` (`theme.json`, `global.css`, `layouts/Base.astro`, `views/*.astro`), resolved per request in `web/src/middleware.ts`: `?theme=` (wins, sets a year-long dated cookie `<name>.<unix seconds>`) → that cookie, *while it is newer than the owner's choice* → `X-Maurice-Theme` (the app's picker, stored in `garden_settings.web_theme`, its date sent as `X-Maurice-Theme-Since`) → `THEME` env → `manuscript`. Shipped garden themes: manuscript, botanical, newsprint, terminal (`default` is the hidden base). Collections: notes, blog, essays, pages, resources (books, articles, movies, series, podcasts, games, people — the cards), fiches (`<slug>-fiche.md`, private garden only). `public` flag = published; `encrypted` notes are encrypted in the built HTML (AES-256-GCM, `PRIVATE_CONTENT_PASSWORD`). Wiki-links `[[slug]]` and typed refs `[text](note:slug)` become links (`remark-cross-ref.mjs`); MOC notes render their children as cards.

**One built engine per household** (since 14 September 2026): `web/dist/server/entry.mjs`, started by `scripts/start-web.sh` on :4321, loopback only; the Bun proxy sends `X-Maurice-Garden`, `X-Maurice-Base`, `X-Maurice-Theme`, `X-Maurice-Theme-Since`, `X-Maurice-Owner`, `X-Maurice-Shared` (all stripped from incoming requests first) and the engine reads them into an `AsyncLocalStorage` (`src/lib/garden-context.ts`). **Gaps**: the private overlay `maurice-web/` is symlinked into `web/src` (use `grep -R`); the activity indicator is transient; the search index is single-locale. Per-garden theme wiring is joined (dated 19 September 2026): a reader's `?theme=` cookie holds only until the owner picks again.

## From specialized Maurices to domains

Until 19 September 2026 a row of `maurices` was a **persona**: a named assistant with its own prompt, model, creativity, a frozen bundle of context and a **hat** chosen from a grid; **Maurice Studio** picked one to summon and the send button's picker switched specialist. That afternoon the design changed: **there is one Maurice**, and what a member used to shape as a second Maurice is a **domain**. The table kept its shape (`services/maurices.ts`, `routes/maurices.ts`: `name`, `tagline`, `prompt`, `model` (null → the member's everyday model), `temp`, `thinking` (null = provider default, true = think first, false = answer directly; offered only on a model the roster marks `optional`), `context_json` (the frozen, baked-in context), `tool_families`, `created_by`) and gained one column, **`kind`**: `domain` (the default; NULL reads as such) — a part of its creator's life, with a brief — or `companion` — a **reading companion**, one book in bound context followed at the reading position with a conduct prompt, an activity with an object, no brief. `hat` and `palette` remain as columns with their old values, but nothing writes or reads them: the apps draw a mark on the member's accent instead. The migration is additive and reversible.

**The one-time sort** (`migrateMauriceKinds()` in `db.ts`): at the first start on a database that predates the column, a row whose bound context is exactly one book on the `progress` scope becomes a companion, anything else a domain; at home *JTTW guide* → companion, *Yi Jing* → domain. The sort never revisits a row; the member re-sorts in the editor or with `PATCH /api/maurices/:id { kind }`. A companion's earlier brief stays in `domain_briefs` but reaches nobody. Ownership, `maurice_access` (how a guest gets what an admin grants), the model preference and the tool families are unchanged; `POST /api/conversations { maurice_id }` still binds a conversation to a row. The **everyday Maurice** is the null row: no bound context, the member's `everyday_model`, the household's `everyday_thinking`.

**In the app** (Mac and iOS): the Studio (`StudioViews.swift`), the summon picker, the hat badge and the hat/colour grids are gone; the send button is Maurice's boater on the member's accent, the header shows one Maurice. In their place the **domains list** (`DomainsViews.swift`, a button beside *New conversation*), the **domain editor** (`DomainEditorView.swift`: name, tagline, Domain / Reading companion, *what it is about*, context, model, creativity, reasoning, tools) and the greeting `DomainGreeting` of a bound conversation. Thirty-two strings changed in the seven languages; `Hats.swift` lives on in the loading animation. **Rooms**: unchanged — Maurice answers when summoned, a room bound at creation still reads that row's prompt and context, and a room **never** gets a brief; nothing in the apps binds a room to a domain any more.

**Maurice Maurice** (since 18 September 2026) is neither a row nor a domain: the built-in specialist of Maurice (`builtinMaurice()`, id `maurice-maurice`), first on every member's `GET /api/maurices` including guests, uneditable and undeletable (403), reached from the domains list under *About Maurice*, boater hat, creativity 0.3, `thinking` false, prompt telling him to answer from the documentation in the asker's language, name the note he draws on, respect "ships vs. exists" and "gaps", and say when the docs are silent. His context is this documentation, read by `services/mauriceDocs.ts` (cached on mtimes) from `MAURICE_DOCS_DIR` if set, else the refreshed set under `~/.maurice/docs/maurice` when at least as new as the bundle, else the repo's `docs/maurice/` (the `scripts/sync-docs.sh` snapshot with its `manifest.json`): the digest plus, in full, any note under the index dated after its `covers` entry; a note marked `internal: true` is skipped by reader and sync script alike. His model is the server's pick (`builtinMauriceModel()`), locked, outside the member's allow-list: Claude Sonnet 4.6 on an Anthropic key, Mistral Medium 3.5 on Scaleway, then Mistral Large, GPT-4o, GLM-5.3, the household default's provider first, never a local model; with no cloud key, the household default. Tool families inherited from the household default. No domain briefs. He goes when the documentation tool (P3-A of the roadmap) replaces him.

**Gaps**: domains are still made by hand (*New domain*) until the night proposes them; a companion's pinned conversation is found (the most recently touched bound conversation, named by `GET /api/domains`), not designed from the book's page; Maurice Maurice's notes reach other households only through a push of the repo and are English only; fine detail lives in the delta or nowhere. Carnet reads none of this until P3-C.

## Domains and their briefs

**What a domain is today**: a row of `maurices` of kind `domain` — a part of its creator's life that Maurice follows, with its name, tagline, statement (`prompt`), bound context, model and tools. A `companion` row is not a domain: no brief, not in the prompt, not on the brief routes. Maurice Maurice is not a row and has no brief; a domain shared with a guest is the creator's domain, not the guest's (`domainsOf` in `services/domainBriefs.ts`, `isDomain` in `services/maurices.ts`).

**The brief**: two or three paragraphs Maurice keeps on the domain, his working memory made visible. One `domain_briefs` row per domain and member (`text`, `updated_at`, `sources_json`, `read_until`, `model` — the night's model, or `member`).

**Written at night** (04:00 local, an hour after the corpus reconciles) or on demand. For every domain of every non-guest member the server gathers what touched it since the last brief — the conversations bound to it (`conversations.maurice_id`), then the corpus's semantic search on name, tagline and the prompt's opening (`corpus__search`, `source_type: conversation`, scoped to the member), then the full-text search on the name; never a conversation bound to another domain, nor one the member said nothing in since; **eight at most**, bound ones first, each an excerpt of ~1 800 characters. With nothing new no model is called. Otherwise the `domain_brief` invocation (DeepSeek V4 Flash, Mistral Small 3.2 in reserve) rewrites from the previous brief and the excerpts: ~300 words for a first brief, ~200 for a rewrite, in the member's language, second person, no title, no list, nothing lent to the member they did not say, no question; 4 000 tokens of room, the text cut at twice the asked length on a paragraph, a reply that hit `max_tokens` is a failure and the previous brief stays; `read_until` moves to the newest message read. A corrected brief is handed to the next rewrite as *the member's* words. Every call is charged to the `system` spender under the night's cap; the run stops at the first domain refused.

**Read in every private conversation.** The everyday Maurice's system prompt carries *Your briefs on {name}'s domains* with every brief of the member's domains, most recently rewritten first, under **3 000 estimated tokens** (`briefsSection`, `BRIEFS_BUDGET_TOKENS`): a brief that does not fit whole is cut at a paragraph and closes the section, and the domains left out are named. It comes after the bound row and the loaded context (so the cached prefix only moves when a brief does) and before the tool roster. **Never in a room**, **never for another member**, not for Maurice Maurice. A brief *in their own words* prevails; Maurice draws on the briefs without reciting them.

**Routes** (`routes/domains.ts`). `GET /api/domains` lists the member's domains and companions as two arrays — a standard member's own rows, the rows granted to a guest (`mine: false`, no brief) — each domain with `brief: { updated_at, model, sources }` or `null`, each companion with `book_id` and the `conversation_id` of its pinned conversation or `null`. Creator only, 404 otherwise, for Maurice Maurice and for a companion: `GET /api/domains/:id/brief` (the brief or `null`, with the name), `PUT` with `{ text }` (stored with `model = member`; empty erases), `DELETE` (idempotent), `POST …/brief/refresh` (rewrite now; `outcome` ∈ `written | unchanged | failed | capped`, 429 when the night's allowance is spent). The admin console's "domain briefs" card shows the last run, counts and cost, and "Rewrite now" (`POST /admin/briefs/run`).

**The domain page in the app** (Mac and iOS, `DomainBriefView.swift`): opened from the domains list or the greeting of a bound conversation. Shows when the brief was last rewritten and by whom (*In your words* once corrected), the text in an editor, and the actions — **Save** (what Maurice reads from the next turn on), **Rewrite now** (outcome said in a line), **Talk about it** (a new conversation bound to the domain, its baked-in context preloaded), **Erase** (confirmed), **Edit** (the domain editor). A companion's row resumes its pinned conversation or starts one; a domain granted to a guest opens a conversation on it, no page. Fifty-odd strings in the seven app languages.

**Ships vs. exists**: the kind, the sort, the list, the editor, the data, the night, the prompt section, the routes and the page are in the server and the Maurice app; nothing depends on the private tools (the corpus ships); the hosted households run the same sort at first start. Carnet has no domain page.

**Gaps**: domains are still created by hand — the mapping that discovers them at night (`tools/corpus/scripts/map_domains.py` exists as a script), the `domains__propose|adjust|adopt` tools and the seeding of the garden are the next sessions; the conversation Maurice opens to propose them exists (see *The chat experience*) but no night calls it yet; a companion's pinned conversation from the book's page is P3-C's; the briefs are the creator's (a guest granted a domain has no brief; a shared brief for a shared domain is open); the budget is estimated (three characters a token) and a correction costs one cache miss; the rewrite's material is found by name, tagline and the prompt's opening, so a one-word domain still pulls in neighbours.

## The context composer

The app's context tray (`Views/ComposerViews.swift`) over `server/src/services/composer/*` and `routes/composer.ts`. Loadable: **notes** (with descendants following `[[wiki-links]]` breadth-first, include-archived, exclusions; `maurice-docs` with descendants is the whole system documentation, seventeen notes), **books** (chapter scope `all` / `up_to` / pick / `progress`; representation `summary` or `full`), **fiches** (with fragments), **past conversations** (summary past 8k tokens, cached against a transcript hash in `conversation_summaries`; `representation: full` pins the transcript; `GET /api/v1/composer/conversations/:id/summary?wait=1`), **files**, **folders**. Two layers: **baked-in** (a persona's `context_json`, locked) and **added** (removable); a third rides beside them since 19 September 2026, invisible in the tray — the member's domain briefs, placed after the loaded context (3 000 tokens at most, private conversations only), edited on the domain page. **Frozen snapshots** (`composer/specs.ts`): the resolved set is frozen at save, the content stays live; refresh to regrow. **Ships**: notes, conversations, files, fiches. **Books** need the experimental Calibre tool. **Gaps**: room context is shared; the budget is a fixed 200k, not the model's window, and loaded context is never trimmed to fit.

## Households, rooms & devices

**A server is one household; a device can hold several; switching member is a tap and a PIN.** First run (`PairingView.swift`): enter a server URL, `GET /api/health` confirms it and returns the household's name, colour and icon; `SessionStore.swift` persists paired households with one session token each; `switchHousehold()` flips the active one. `UserPickerView.swift`: PIN login (`POST /api/auth/login` with `user_id` + `pin`), admin login (username + password), or an invite code (`POST /api/auth/enroll`, then `/api/auth/set-pin`; guests need no PIN). Invite codes: 8 characters, one active per member, ~7 days. **Roles**: admin, standard, guest — a guest's reach is `guest_contacts`, checked both directions (`guestCanReach()`), plus the domains granted (`maurice_access`). Orthogonal to the role, a member can be marked a **child** (`users.is_child`, a box on the console's member page): Maurice never opens a conversation for a child, and the night proposes nothing to them. **Rooms**: a 1:1 is a room with one human; `addParticipant()` enforces guest reach; in a room 💬 (human-only) is the default button and Return posts it, ➤ summons Maurice with ⌘Return; read state per participant; the foyer's unread roll-up also counts a conversation Maurice opened that you have not opened yet. The per-user channel (`/api/me/ws`) carries `activity`, `conversation_added` and `conversation_opened`. **A conversation Maurice opens** is guarded by the household: never a child, never a guest, never twice within `households.maurice_opens_min_days` (fifteen by default, "Days between two conversations Maurice opens" in the console); the operator's route can pass `force`, the night never will. **Moderation**: long-press → Report or Block in shared rooms only; reports at `/api/reports`, child-safety first. **Push**: APNs (`push.ts`, `apns.ts`) for a room's activity, an invitation, or a conversation Maurice opened ("Maurice: …"); tokens with `household_tag`.

**The household archive** (since 19 September 2026): a versioned `.maurice.tar.gz` (`maurice-archive` v1, `services/archive.ts`, format in `docs/household-archive.md`) holding everything the household *is* — `maurice.db` and the data-api databases as `VACUUM INTO` snapshots, the gardens with each member's git history and `gardens.json`, images, files, uploads, avatars, `config.toml`, and a `manifest.json` (household, members, schema and server version, contents). Left out: backups, logs, the corpus vector index (regenerable), `.env`, the Mac's `ops/` secrets, the bare remotes under `~/.maurice/git`. It contains the provider keys: **it is a secret**. Export: the console's section 07 "Export this household" (`GET /admin/export`) or a bearer token on `GET /api/admin/export`, streamed. Import into an empty data directory (refused if a `maurice.db` is there): `scripts/import-household.sh <archive> [dir]` on a Mac, `scripts/container.sh import <archive>` for a local container, `ops/household.sh add <host> <name> <domain> --from <archive>` on a shared host (over ssh's stdin, no copy stays). Still by hand after: the corpus index and the gardens' `origin` remotes.

**Gaps**: single-tenant server; the `/api/auth/pair` device ceremony is implemented but unused; sessions don't expire; a guest's access to domains has no override.

A fiche is *opened* by a deliberate gesture; an article share writes one with `meta.opened: false` until the reader writes on it.

**Articles pipeline** (ships): `POST /api/v1/garden/articles`, called by Carnet's share sheet, the clipper and the garden tool; accepts the browser's rendered DOM; de-duplicates under `rel=canonical`; a site refusing robots yields a **bookmark** fiche (`status: needs-capture`) completed by a later browser share; AI summary generated after the response into the frontmatter.

**Highlights & résonances** (ship with Carnet): highlights in SQLite per member; a résonance is a dated block under `## Résonances` on the target's fiche with a literal `[[wiki-link]]` — no link table, the markdown is the truth.

**Flashcards** (ship, September 2026): generated on demand from a chapter, a book, a fiche or a fragment, highlights covered first; one file per pass in the **Obsidian Spaced Repetition** syntax with the plugin's `<!--SR:…-->` schedule; frontmatter holds the source hash (stale detection) and a fingerprint per card (hand-edited cards survive regeneration); `_cards/` is git-ignored; language French by default, `cards_lang` on the fiche, `lang`/`answer_lang` per call, a *vocabulary* mode; `/api/v1/garden/cards`.

**Corpus** (`tools/corpus`, ships): RAG over reading and conversations — `search`, `search_by_tags`, `search_by_author`, `search_in_book`, `search_book_summaries`, `get_chunk_context`, `index_conversation` (one, or every one in the background), `reconcile_status`, `prune`; indexes notes, fiches, fragments and cards as they change (the server pushes writes; a watcher in the gateway catches the rest); vector store **sqlite-vec**, one file per member under `tools/corpus/data/vectors/` (`<member>.db`, `_default.db` for shared content — books, dossiers); `import_chat_export` ingests Claude and ChatGPT export zips ("bring your history"). **Embedding model since 19 September 2026: the Qwen3-Embedding family at 1024 dimensions** (`nomic-embed-text` before it put every French conversation in one block): **0.6B through Ollama at home, `qwen3-embedding-8b` on Scaleway for a hosted instance** — matryoshka-trained, so every size is asked for the same `dimensions` and a store has one shape. `corpus_meta` pins the model and width that wrote a file and the store **refuses to open** under another (`sqlite_vec_store.py`, `_check_pin`); a model change is a full re-index into a fresh store (`python -m src.main index --force`, then `--source conversations`; the home corpus, 125 000 chunks, takes a night on the 0.6B), the old store kept beside it (`tools/corpus/data-nomic-embed-text/`). Config is `vector_size` plus optional `query_prefix` / `document_prefix`. Hosted households installed before this keep `bge-multilingual-gemma2` at 3584. **Live conversations**: the after-reply push (`indexConversationInBackground`) had called the tool by its bare name and the gateway's "Unknown tool" came back as text, so no conversation since the June import had entered the index; the call now goes through `corpusCall` (prefixed, refusals logged), the backlog was reconciled by hand, and the nightly reconcile is the safety net.

**Tracks** (`tools/tracks`, experimental): `request_deep_research`, `get_research_status`, `generate_briefing`, `get_latest_briefing`, `get_dossier`, `get_dossier_tree`, `list_dossiers`, `trigger_signal_report`; SQLite storage; findings can be published to the garden.

**Gaps**: no temporal mirror; flashcards have no backup; the nightly does not re-walk the file sources (the gateway's watcher alone notices a garden file changed while the server was down); the shared pool answers without the books until the re-index reaches them; tracks' deep research is the least tested.

## Applications, plugins, and the case of the mail

A parked product thread (17 September 2026): how a personal application such as mail triage attaches to Maurice without the product reading as a pile of experiments. "Plugin" conflates maturity (experimental) and architecture (extension); mail is an extension and no longer experimental. The generic pattern worth extracting: a closed vocabulary as the containment boundary, a proposal before any write, deterministic rules before the model, an audit log, failures that announce themselves. What is Candide's: the four-state GTD method, the topic labels, the horizons, the example config being the real one. Sovereignty for mail means handing over keys, not a copy: no send, no delete, no arbitrary move, every write logged; the Anthropic escalation impossible by construction on a sovereign instance.
