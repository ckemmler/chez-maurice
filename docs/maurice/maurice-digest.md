# Maurice — the digest

A condensed reference to the system documentation ([[maurice-docs]] and the notes under it), rewritten 19 September 2026 and brought up to date on 20 September, the day memory was rebuilt in three storeys. Each section states what a part *is*, the names a person needs (routes, files, scripts, variables), what ships versus what only exists, and the known gaps. The history of how each thing came to be is in the full notes; this keeps the facts. The `covers` map in the frontmatter names the note dates this digest reflects: a note updated since is read in full beside it. This is what the documentation tool `maurice_docs` loads when a member asks Maurice about Maurice; a note marked `internal: true` is never part of it.

Two distinctions run through everything. **Ships vs. exists**: the public product is chat + the garden + corpus search; the wider capabilities (Calibre, tasks, coaching, health, tracks, mail…) live in the private `maurice-tools` overlay, are in daily use at home, and are *experimental*. **Vision vs. built**: the founding vision ([[maurice]]) describes a household AI with a tamed, visible memory; these docs describe what is built and flag the gaps.

## Concept — the why

Maurice is a self-hosted **household AI assistant** with a scoped, visible memory: the *garden*, a folder of Markdown notes per member, git-backed, that grows only by deliberate human action — nothing enters it without an explicit request, with one asked-for exception since 19 September 2026 (the notes Maurice seeds on a domain, marked unreviewed until the member keeps them). "Apprivoisé" (tamed) is the stance: the member chooses exactly what Maurice knows for a conversation. **There is one Maurice**; the *domains* of a member's life are what he follows closely, each with a brief he keeps and the member can read, correct or erase. Since the night of 19 September 2026 the domains **emerge**: Maurice maps a member's conversations at night, proposes domains in a conversation he opens himself, and makes nothing a domain without the member's explicit yes. Sovereignty means the data stays where the household put it: no cloud account, no analytics, no email; and since September 2026 a single European supplier, Scaleway, can provide both the machine and the model, so nothing need leave the EU. "Small on purpose": the right-sized win is a few hundred households, used by people who are not its author.

Vision items **not built**: the "temporal mirror" (automatic daily/weekly reviews) and Maurice noticing patterns and proposing notes on his own (not intended either — the seeded notes are asked for twice, and marked). Emergent hats derived from the active note subtree are closed by another route: a domain *is* the scope, proposed from the conversations rather than derived from a folder. The **domains** design (the owner's French note *maurice-domaines*: domains that emerge, each with a visible brief, proposed in a conversation Maurice opens, the garden seeded on consent, a history imported so the map exists sooner) is built through P0–P4 of its roadmap, all on 19 September 2026 — see *Domains and their briefs*. Two further concept notes sit under the index: *What I'm actually building for* (the pace and the right-sized win) and *Applications, plugins, and the case of the mail* (a parked product thread).

## Architecture — the five parts

- **Maurice app** (`app/Maurice`, SwiftUI, one target for iPhone/iPad/Mac): the conversation client. Holds no authoritative state; pairs to a server, authenticates a member, talks HTTPS + WebSocket. Dictation, cost meter, composer; one Maurice and, beside the conversations, the domains list with their briefs; the import of a ChatGPT or Claude history in its settings.
- **Carnet** (separate repo `carnet/`, iOS/iPadOS): the pocket companion for capturing and reading — log signals, read books and articles, browse the garden, save what you read via a share sheet; since 19 September 2026 a Domains shelf and an APNs registration of its own.
- **Server** (`server/`, one Bun process on Hono): `server/src` is the chat engine (auth, users, conversations, domains and reading companions, files, gardens, composer, models, reports, the streaming agentic loop, the three nightly jobs, the documentation tool, the import); `server/data-api` is the personal-data layer at `/api/v1/*` (health, tasks, signals, tracks, coaching, Calibre, bank, layouts, places, uploads, and the garden's media: articles, entries, résonances, flashcards).
- **Tools** (`tools/`): Python MCP servers behind one `mcp_gateway`, reached by the server over loopback. Public: `garden`, `corpus`, `shared`, `mcp_gateway`. Private overlay (`maurice-tools` repo, symlinked): tracks, health, signals, tasks, calendar, contacts, calibre, readwise, compte, social, layouts, thoughts, mail, pipelines. Five *native* tools live in the server's own loop: `maurice_docs` and the four `domains__*`.
- **Web** (`web/`, Astro): renders each member's garden as a themeable site under `/g/<member>`, and the public site (candide.me) on Cloudflare Pages.
- Plus a **browser clipper** (`clients/web-clipper`) that saves the rendered DOM of a page as an article fiche.

**A message's path.** `POST /api/conversations/:id/messages` → stored and fanned to the room over WebSocket (in a multi-human room Maurice replies only when summoned with `@maurice`/`@claude`) → system prompt → history bounded to the model's window → tools filtered by families → streamed request with cache breakpoints → NDJSON `StreamEvent`s (`text_delta`, `thinking`, `ping`, `tool_call`, `tool_data`, `usage`, `done`, `error`) → tools fed back, up to 6 rounds → the app renders prose live and data cards from the data itself. The turn belongs to the conversation, not the request (`GET …/turn` re-attaches). Details in *The server*.

**Delivery: a container, and only a container** (decided 14 September 2026; the notarized `.pkg` is retired). `infra/container/Dockerfile` has two targets: `dev` (bind-mounted sources, Calibre, the private overlays) and `production` (sources copied in, 2 MCP tools — garden, corpus — no Calibre, ~2 GB, behind Caddy with Let's Encrypt; the build fails on a dangling symlink rather than ship a private repo). Base image Ubuntu 26.04 (arm64). `scripts/deploy.sh <host> [tag]` builds on the Mac, pushes through a registry (`MAURICE_REGISTRY`) or pipes over ssh, and recreates the households; `infra/cloud-init/maurice.yaml` prepares a machine; `infra/container/MULTI-HOUSEHOLD.md` + `ops/household.sh` run several households on one machine as compose projects behind one Caddy, each on a loopback admin port from 3101 up. `scripts/container.sh` drives the local container (`seed` snapshots the live SQLite databases with `VACUUM INTO`; `import <archive>` starts one from a household archive). The entrypoint symlinks the repo-relative state (`data/`, `logs/`, `tools/corpus/data`) into the data volume. No cron in the container: the nightly jobs live in the server process. `scripts/provision-member.ts` creates what `createUser` does not (the garden directory, the `gardens.json` entry).

**A household costs ~260 MB idle** (measured 14 September 2026), so an 8 GB machine carries about twenty. **The home Mac is the exception**: it still runs launchd agents (`scripts/service.sh`): `com.maurice.api` (port 3001), `com.maurice.mcp-gateway`, `com.maurice.web`, nightly `com.maurice.backup`, and one `com.maurice.household.<name>` per extra household; it reads `~/.maurice/config.toml` and `~/.maurice/.env`. A local container runs beside it on :13001 on a copy of the data.

**The first rented host, `maurice-fleet`** (since 17 September 2026): a Scaleway BASIC2-A2C-8G in Paris (2 ARM vCPU, 8 GB, 51.15.217.40) carrying Aline's household and the App Review one, both thinking on Scaleway end to end; `MAURICE_REGISTRY=rg.fr-par.scw.cloud/maurice scripts/deploy.sh maurice-fleet` is the whole update (an already-built tag is the rollback). Everything Scaleway sits in a project `maurice` with one IAM key limited to Generative APIs and the registry (`~/.maurice/ops/scaleway-fleet.env`). The demo fleet (throwaway instances, TTL a couple of weeks, a CC0 seed household) is the planned sibling, the answer to the cold-start problem.

**Networking** is the operator's plumbing, not Maurice's: Tailscale (private mesh, at home) or a Cloudflare Tunnel (public hostname). Trap: `brew services restart cloudflared` drops `tunnel run` from the plist.

**Gaps**: git is assumed present on the host (the server shells out to system `git`); the vector store lives in the repo tree (`tools/corpus/data`) rather than the data dir; no automatic provider failover; TLS on the Mac off by default. The architecture note's own "still open" list is partly stale (it still names `astro dev` in production and the `.pkg`'s future, both settled).

## The server

`server/index.ts` mounts both halves. Listens on `PORT`, serves generated images and avatars, proxies `/mcp/*` to the gateway and `/g/<member>/` to the garden engine (both hardcoded to `127.0.0.1`), upgrades WebSockets for rooms (`roomBus.ts`: a per-room channel and a per-user channel at `/api/me/ws`).

**Chat-engine routes** (`server/src/routes`): `/api/auth` (setup, login, enroll, invite/pairing codes) · `/api/users` (roster, profiles, avatars, preferences, API tokens, guest contacts, `POST /me/device-token`) · `/api/conversations` (threads and rooms, messages, streaming replies, `GET /:id/turn`, `POST /:id/turn/stop`, `POST /:id/read`, `PATCH /:id/maurice`, participants, tool-family overrides, `/search`) · `/api/maurices` (the rows of `maurices` — domains and reading companions, `kind` on `POST`/`PATCH`) · `/api/domains` (`GET /`; `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /:id/brief`, `POST /:id/brief/refresh`) · `/api/import` (`POST /?provider=anthropic|chatgpt`, `GET /status?job=`, `GET /history?provider=`) · `/api/admin/conversations/open` · `/api/admin/domains/map` · `/api/admin/domains/proposals` · `/api/admin/corpus/reconcile` · `/api/files` · `/api/v1/gardens` · `/api/v1/composer` · `/api/models` · `/api/tool-families` · `/api/reports` · `/api/ancillary` (loopback-only model turns for the Python tools) · `/api/me/usage`, `/api/admin/usage` · `/api/admin/export` (the household archive; `GET /admin/export` behind the console cookie) · `/api/admin/status` · `/admin`, `/login` (the admin web UI: members with the child box and the import, models, provider keys, Calibre, access grid, caps, ancillary pins, the corpus, briefs, mapping and docs cards — `POST /admin/corpus/reconcile`, `/admin/briefs/run`, `/admin/mapping/run`, `/admin/docs/refresh`, `/admin/users/:id/import*` — the export). **Data-api routes** under `/api/v1/*`: `health/*`, `signals`, `coaching`, `tasks`, `tracks`, `dossiers`, `layouts`, `dashboard`, `places`, `uploads`, `compte`, `bank-transactions`; `garden-tools/*` (the owner's toolbar, `review-state` and `review-note` among them); `calibre/*`, `books`; `garden/articles`, `garden/entries`, `garden/links`, `garden/cards`; `articles/scrape` (legacy alias).

**The turn registry** (`services/turns.ts`, since 19 September 2026). A turn streams NDJSON `StreamEvent`s (`thinking` throttled to one a second, text never forwarded; `ping` after 15 s of silence; `usage` once before `done`). The reply in flight is kept in memory per conversation, so a client that vanishes no longer stops it: the reply runs to the end, is persisted and fanned out over the room socket. `GET /api/conversations/:id/turn` re-attaches: `204` when nothing is known, else a `resume` line then the live events; a finished turn stays reachable 60 s. `POST …/turn/stop` aborts and keeps what streamed. One reply at a time per conversation: a second summons is `409`.

**The agentic loop** (`streamResponse` in `services/claude.ts`): system prompt (household, profile, the bound domain or companion, composer context, then — private conversation only, never in a room — the member's domain briefs under 3 000 tokens, the one-sentence import hint (`importHintSection`), in a conversation Maurice opened the proposals section, then a *Your tools* section naming exactly the families present, the safety floor last) → messages (the clock as a trailing system reminder, never in the system prompt, so the prefix stays cacheable; a conversation Maurice opened gets a constant user lead before his first message; an assistant turn that called tools carries a *tool trail* — six results at most, 240 characters each) → model and provider → MCP tools sorted by name, the native tools appended after them → **context-window fit** (`contextWindow.ts`: the model's `ctx` less the head, a reply reserve and a margin; over that the oldest turns are dropped to 70% of budget with hysteresis, the cut stored as `context_from`, the model told the beginning is missing) → stream; tool-use blocks run (web search via Tavily, the documentation sub-turn, a `domains__*` call, or an MCP `callTool`), results minified and fed back; Stop reaches the provider on every path and is checked between tool calls; an OpenAI-style round silent for 180 s is abandoned; **`MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 6`**. In a multi-human room Maurice answers only when mentioned (`mentionsMaurice()`); ➤ posts `{ summon: true, maurice_id }`, 💬 a human-only message. Every model request logs a `[round]` line, every tool call a `[tool]` line.

**Prompt caching.** Anthropic: three explicit breakpoints (last system block, end of history, the tool-result trail), a cached read at a tenth. Z.ai and Scaleway cache implicitly; Mistral and OpenAI only with a `prompt_cache_key` (the conversation id; `PROMPT_CACHE_KEY_PROVIDERS`). On Scaleway only DeepSeek V4 Flash reports cached tokens (priced at 0.2).

**Providers** (chosen per resolved model's `models.provider`, no failover): **Anthropic** (native loop, key `households.api_key`); **Ollama** (local, `ollama.ts`, a 32k window requested); **OpenAI / Mistral / Z.ai / Scaleway** through one Chat-Completions door, `openaiChat.ts` (`isOpenAIStyle()`, `openaiStyleBaseUrl()`, `openaiStyleKey()` in `claude.ts`; a project-scoped Scaleway key needs `api.scaleway.ai/<project>/v1`, hence `scaleway_project_id`). Reasoning models stream thinking under `reasoning_content` or `reasoning`. Echo mode with no key at all. **A key is what makes a model exist**: `configuredProviders()` gates the apps' roster, the admin grid (callable models only; hidden rows carried over on save) and the ancillary pins. Seeded rosters: Anthropic (Claude Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5), OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini), Mistral (Large, Small), Z.ai (GLM-5.3 text-only, GLM-5.3 Flash with `vision`, 1M windows), Scaleway (ten Serverless models: Mistral Small 3.2 — the Scaleway everyday default, reads images — Gemma 4 26B, Qwen 3.6 35B, GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek V4 Flash, Qwen 3.5 397B, Qwen 3 235B, Llama 3.3 70B, Mistral Medium 3.5, GLM 5.2). `pricing.ts` prices every seeded model (Scaleway in euros at a fixed rate); an unpriced model prices at `null`, never zero.

**The reasoning switch** (since 18 September 2026): `models.thinking` ∈ `none` / `optional` / `always`; a row of `maurices` records its choice (`maurices.thinking`), the household one for the everyday Maurice (`households.everyday_thinking`, admin console only); each provider path translates it (**Z.ai `reasoning_effort: low|high`** — the earlier `thinking: {type: disabled}` was refused outright from 20 September with code 1210, *"This model always engages in thinking and cannot be disabled; please use low, high, or max"*: GLM-5.3 and its Flash always reason and only the effort is adjustable, `low` being as close to off as they go, a couple of dozen reasoning tokens against more than a thousand on `max`; Anthropic 4.6+ `thinking: {type: adaptive|disabled}`, Ollama `think`). Scaleway's reasoning models are `always` and ignore it.

**Ancillary models** (`services/ancillary.ts`, since 13 September 2026): everything that calls a model and is not the chat — summaries, flashcards, signal parsing, the tools' classifiers, the nights' functions, the documentation sub-turn — is an *invocation*, resolved pin (`ancillary_models`) → household `ancillary_model` → chat default, skipping models whose provider has no key. Each tier (light/standard/heavy) has a European preferred list: Scaleway first (Mistral Small 3.2, GPT-OSS 120B, Qwen 3.5 397B), then Mistral; Anthropic and OpenAI out by decision, Z.ai by size. `domain_brief`, `domain_mapping` and `domain_seed` carry their own `prefer` list: DeepSeek V4 Flash, then Mistral Small 3.2 on Scaleway, then Mistral's own Small. `maurice_docs` carries a **computed default** instead of advice (`docsModel()` in `mauriceDocs.ts`: a strong cloud model of the household's own provider — Sonnet 4.6, Mistral Medium 3.5 on Scaleway, Mistral Large, GPT-4o, GLM 5.3 — never a local one), overridden by a pin. Pins record `source` (`auto` follows the advice; `admin` never moves); `ancillary_advised` remembers which invocations were ever offered advice; `pinNewInvocations()` pins new ones at every start; a deleted pin is never re-created. The Python tools call `POST /api/ancillary` (loopback only, `middleware/loopback.ts`) through `tools/shared/model_config.complete()`; a turn reports its usage, and a request may name an explicit `model` for an experiment.

**The spending fuse** (`budget.ts`): a no-op unless a cap is set; it *refuses* where `pricing.ts` *measures*. **Three layers, the tightest wins**: the instance's env caps (`MAURICE_SPEND_CAP_USD` lifetime, `MAURICE_SPEND_CAP_DAILY_USD` rolling 24 h; 10 USD daily on the fleet host) ≥ the household's daily cap (`households.spend_cap_daily_usd`) ≥ the member's (`users.spend_cap_daily_usd`). Checked at the top of every agentic round; refuses an unpriced model under any cap; Ollama exempt by provider; the refusal names the closest cap and emits usage then an `error` event. Spend is written to `spend_ledger` by `addMessage` with `user_id`. **A fourth spender, the night**: the briefs', the mapping's and an adoption's first brief carry `user_id = 'system'` under their own allowance `households.spend_cap_system_daily_usd` ("Night's daily allowance"), weighed where a member's cap would be, checked *before* each call; the real cost is recorded whether the result was kept or not; a cap that blows stops the night. **Two ancillary calls are charged to a member**: the documentation sub-turn and the garden seeding (`recordSpend(usage, memberId)`). `GET /api/me/usage` returns `today_usd`, `month_usd`, `cap_daily_usd`, `remaining_usd`; `GET /api/admin/usage` returns everyone's plus the night.

**Auth**: admin created at `POST /api/auth/setup`, logs in with username + password; standard members with `user_id` + a mandatory PIN (rate-limited per IP); guests enroll without one; `POST /api/auth/enroll` redeems an invite code (rate-limited per IP and globally); pairing tokens are the one-shot programmatic path. Opaque session tokens; `maur_*` API tokens scoped `mcp` / `health` / `full` (`health` opens only `/healthz`'s full face; `mcp` is not enforced). Roles `admin` / `standard` / `guest`. Image references in a message body are confined to the images directory (`resolveImagePath`). **The admin dashboard is loopback-only** on three signals: loopback socket peer (Bun's `requestIP`), loopback `Host`, no Cloudflare edge header — a hosted household's `/admin` is a permanent 403, reached through an ssh forward (`ops/admin.ts <name>`).

**Health & ops**: `GET /healthz` public (`status`, `service`, `version`) or, with a token, `git_sha`, `built_at`, `schema_version`, `uptime_s`, `db` (a `quick_check` cached five minutes), `disk_free_mb`, `errors_1h` / `errors_24h` / `last_error_at` / `last_error_kind`; 503 when degraded. Version from `git describe` first, then `server/build-info.json` (`scripts/build-info.sh`); `MAURICE_VERSION` / `MAURICE_GIT_SHA` / `MAURICE_BUILT_AT` override (`services/buildInfo.ts`). Operator tooling in `ops/`: `fleet.yaml`, `fleet-status.ts`, `tower.ts` (TUI: `d` deploys, `R` restarts, `a` opens an admin console), `admin.ts`, `recreate-container.sh`, `mint-health-token.ts`, `backup.sh`; no discovery, no central registry, no fleet UI.

**Documentation refresh** (`services/mauriceDocsRefresh.ts`, since 19 September 2026): `MAURICE_DOCS_URL` names where the documentation tool's notes are published (default the repo's `docs/maurice/` on `main`; `off` disables). 20 s after boot, then every 24 h, the server GETs `manifest.json` (the SSRF guard; `MAURICE_DOCS_ALLOW_LOCAL=1` lifts it); if `generated_at` is newer, it downloads each note whose sha256 differs, stages the set and swaps it into `~/.maurice/docs/maurice`; any failure leaves the previous set. `docsDir()` chooses `MAURICE_DOCS_DIR` if set, else the refreshed set when at least as new as the bundle, else the bundle. `GET /api/admin/status` carries `docs: { source, generated_at, last_check_at, last_error }`; the console's *Maurice's documentation* card has "Check now".

**Three nights, one shape.** Each is a service in the server process (the container has no cron, and the gateway is the only writer to the stores): a tick every ten minutes, once per local day, so a server down at the hour catches up; the last run in a JSON file under the app dir; a run in flight shared, not doubled; an `off` variable (and `NODE_ENV=test`) to disable; a card in the console with the last run, counts, cost and a button.

- **03:00 — the corpus reconciles** (`services/corpusNightly.ts`, `corpus-nightly.json`, `MAURICE_CORPUS_NIGHTLY`): every conversation (`corpus__index_conversation` with no id — a hash diff per message) as a background task polled through `corpus__reconcile_status` (three hours at most), then a prune per member of the file-backed entries whose file is gone and, on the first member's call, the shared pool (`corpus__prune`, `shared: true`). "Reconcile now" = `POST /admin/corpus/reconcile`; `POST /api/admin/corpus/reconcile` for a script.
- **04:00 — the domain briefs** (`services/domainBriefs.ts`, `domain-briefs-nightly.json`, `MAURICE_DOMAIN_BRIEFS`): mechanics in *Domains and their briefs*. "Rewrite now" = `POST /admin/briefs/run`.
- **05:00 — the domain mapping** (`services/domainMapping.ts`, `domain-mapping-nightly.json`, `MAURICE_DOMAIN_MAPPING`): mechanics and thresholds in *Domains and their briefs*. "Map now" = `POST /admin/mapping/run`; `POST /api/admin/domains/map` for one member, `dry_run` and `force`.

**Backups.** *Hosted households* (since 19 September 2026): `infra/host/backup.sh` runs from the host's crontab at 03:30 UTC — a `VACUUM INTO` copy of every SQLite database, then restic sends the volume to one repository per host on Scaleway Object Storage (bucket `maurice-fleet-backups`), 14 daily / 8 weekly / 12 monthly kept, encrypted with a password only the Mac holds (`~/.maurice/ops/fleet-backup.env`). `ops/backup.sh restore-test <host> <name>` rehearses a restore into a throwaway household; `ops/backup.sh restore` does it for real. *The Mac*: `com.maurice.backup` snapshots `maurice.db` and `life.db` nightly into `~/.maurice/backups/db` (`scripts/backup-db.sh`); gardens rely on git; `compte.db`, `recommendations.db` and the git-ignored flashcards are not covered.

**Gaps**: above the fuse nothing yet — no balance, no prepaid quota, no statement a customer can read; the ancillary calls made *for a member* other than the docs sub-turn and the seeding (summaries, flashcards, signal parses) have no spender; the docs sub-turn's cost is in the ledger but not in the turn's own usage figure; Scaleway's cache invisible on nine models out of ten; the turn registry is memory only; the tool trail is plain text in history; no failover; a moved household keeps stale local models; context estimated by characters; the Mac's backup is partial; `mcp` scope unenforced; an error collector undecided; the tracks data-api routes carry no member check.

## The data model

One SQLite file, `maurice.db` (WAL), in `~/.maurice/`; the whole schema is `server/src/db.ts` — `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` plus append-only guarded `ALTER`s and idempotent backfills, no migration framework, `PRAGMA user_version` stamped by `SCHEMA_VERSION`. The data-api keeps its own files under `~/.maurice/data/` (`MAURICE_DATA_DIR`): `life.db` (highlights, reading positions, health, tasks, signals, dossiers, coaching, layouts, places), `compte.db`, `recommendations.db`; plus the corpus vectors and the Calibre library.

- **Identity**: `households` (one row `id='default'`: the provider keys `api_key`, `openai_api_key`, `mistral_api_key`, `zai_api_key`, `scaleway_api_key` + `scaleway_project_id`, `fal_api_key`; `default_model`, `ancillary_model`, `max_tokens`, `everyday_thinking`, `spend_cap_daily_usd`, `spend_cap_system_daily_usd` (the night's allowance), `maurice_opens_min_days` (null = fifteen), `ollama_host`, `default_tool_families`, `color`, `icon`, the `*_seeded` guards); `users` (`username` is also the garden directory; `role`, `pin_hash`, `profile_text`, `experimental_tools`, `everyday_model`, `spend_cap_daily_usd`, `is_child` — a box, not a birth date); `user_preferences` (theme, font, density, palette, locale).
- **Auth & devices**: `sessions`, `api_tokens` (`token_hash`, `scope`), `invite_codes`, `device_tokens` (APNs, with `platform` — `carnet-ios` for Carnet — and `household_tag`), `devices` (pairing, unused by the app), `guest_contacts`.
- **Conversations**: `conversations` (`maurice_id` null = everyday, `origin` `'anthropic'` / `'chatgpt'` for imports, `imported_at` = the wall-clock of the import, `tool_families`, `context_from`, `opened_by` ∈ `member`/`maurice` — `maurice` for a conversation he opened, by the operator's hand or by the night; such a conversation is where the `domains__*` tools exist, and is never read by the mapping); `messages` (`author_id`, `maurice_id`, `model`, `data` = frozen tool results for the data cards, `usage`); `messages_fts` (FTS5, trigger-maintained); `conversation_summaries` (keyed on a transcript hash); `conversation_participants` (`last_read_at`); `reports`; `spend_ledger` (`at`, `provider`, `model`, `cost_usd`, `user_id`; `user_id = 'system'` is the night — no row in `users`; a deleted message never un-spends).
- **Files**: `folders`, `files` (bytes at `{dataDir}/files/{id}{ext}`).
- **Gardens**: no `gardens` table — `note_shares` (owner, slug, member) and `garden_settings` (audience key → `web_theme`); the content is Markdown under `~/.maurice/gardens/<username>/`, one git repo per member.
- **Domains & composer**: `maurices` (`kind` = `domain` — the default, NULL reads as such — or `companion`, sorted once at first start by `migrateMauriceKinds()`; `hat` and `palette` are the personas' leftovers, written and read by nothing; `thinking` nullable 0/1; `name`, `tagline`, `prompt`, `model`, `temp`, `context_json`, `tool_families`, `created_by`); `domain_briefs` (`maurice_id` + `member_id` PK, both cascade; `text`, `updated_at`, `sources_json` = the conversations the last rewrite read, `read_until` = the newest message it saw, `model` = what wrote it, `member` when the member corrected it); `domain_proposals` (`id`, `member_id`, `name`, `summary`, `conversation_ids_json`, `state` ∈ `proposed` / `adopted` / `dismissed` / `expired` / `superseded`, `presented` 0/1 — the three the opening message shows —, `conversation_id` = the conversation Maurice opened to carry them, `maurice_id` once adopted, `stats_json` = the numbers behind the verdict (size, months active, conversations in 90 and 365 days, cohesion, `verdict` alive or lived, the model's `is_domain` and `split_hint`, `origin`); a dismissed proposal's conversations are never mapped again); `maurice_access`; `composer_specs` (keyed conversation + account); both `context_json` and `spec_json` are frozen resolved sets. Conversations once bound to `maurice-maurice` were unbound once at start.
- **Models**: `models` (`tier`, `vendor`, `provider`, `ctx` in k tokens, `vision`, `thinking` ∈ `none`/`optional`/`always` — Ollama fills it from `/api/show`; prices are in `pricing.ts`, not here), `user_model_access` (standard members' allow-list; admins computed-all), `ancillary_models` (`invocation`, `model_id`, `source`), `ancillary_advised` (`invocation`, `at`).
- **Bridge**: `calibre_libraries` (one global library, set in the admin).

Tool families resolve **conversation → bound row → household default → tier default**. **Gaps**: single household per server; room context shared not per-participant; the ledger counts money not tokens (an unpriced model records nothing) and most ancillary calls made on a member's behalf are charged to nobody; hand-rolled migrations (the guest-role rebuild of `users` once dropped `everyday_model` and `experimental_tools`, fixed 19 September 2026 with a test); test isolation by environment (lazy imports).

## The MCP tool ecosystem

`tools/mcp_gateway/server.py` discovers every tool server under `tools/` at startup (three shapes: `gateway_context`, a module-level `app`, or a FastMCP `mcp`), namespaces tools as `{server}__{tool}` (`garden__create_note`, `signals__signal_log`), serves a unified endpoint and per-server ones over Streamable HTTP. Per-member context comes from the `X-Maurice-Member-Id` header into a contextvar (`tools/shared/context.py`). Auth: a bearer token (`MAURICE_MCP_TOKEN`) for the loopback caller, and OAuth 2.1 / PKCE (`oauth.py`) accepting `maur_*` tokens. `tools/shared/model_config.py` resolves an invocation's model from `ancillary_models` read-only.

The engine's client is `server/src/services/mcpClient.ts`, through the `/mcp/*` proxy. **Tool families** gate the roster: the tier default is empty for every model; `web`, `signals` and — since 20 September 2026 — **`corpus`** are always on (a memory you have to remember to switch on is not one: it was experimental, off by default, and in none of the owner's conversations, so a question falling squarely into a domain could not be followed up although the index held the answer). The corpus carries two guards no other always-on family needs: it is **member-private** (`PRIVATE_ONLY`), withheld the moment a conversation has a second participant, since the turn is taken for whoever spoke and an unguarded search would read their private conversations out to the room; and only part of it is handed over — its **nine writing tools** (index, prune, reindex, import, map) are never offered to a model at all, and a turn that did not ask for the family by name gets `corpus__search` and `corpus__get_chunk_context` alone, hence `selectedFamilies` beside `resolveFamilies`, *the turn chose this family* and *the turn holds it* having stopped meaning the same thing; `garden` is sub-split into `garden-notes`, `garden-journal`, `garden-people`, `garden-fragments`, `garden-media`, `garden-publish`, `garden-other` (`services/toolFamilies.ts`); a `domains` family is `core`. Everything but `web`, `signals`, `garden-notes`, `garden-journal` is **experimental** and hidden until an admin ticks *experimental tools* on the member. The system prompt names only the families actually present (`toolRosterNotice()`), web search only when Tavily is configured, image generation only when a fal key exists.

**The documentation tool — `maurice_docs`** (`server/src/services/mauriceDocsTool.ts`, wired in `claude.ts`, since the evening of 19 September 2026, P3-A): a native tool in **every roster**, for every member, guests included, whatever the families say — a member may always ask Maurice about Maurice — and the *Your tools* section says such questions go to it, never to memory. Given a `question`, it runs a **sub-turn**: one `ancillaryComplete` on the `maurice_docs` invocation whose system prompt says to ground everything in the notes, name the note, say what is experimental or not built, answer in the question's language, followed by this digest and the notes newer than its `covers` (`docsForContext()` in `mauriceDocs.ts`, `internal: true` never); the answer comes back as the tool result, prose, and Maurice relays it in his own voice. Given a `note` (a slug: `server`, `domains`, `carnet`…), it returns that note **in full**, no model call. The sub-turn's system prompt is byte-stable and asks the provider to cache it (`cacheSystem`), so the ~22 000 tokens of documentation are read at a tenth the second time. Measured: a first question ~8 ¢ on Sonnet 4.6, a note-in-full answer 0.13 ¢, the next questions 1.6–2.5 ¢, a conversation that never asks about Maurice pays nothing. Every roster now holds at least one tool, so an Ollama model that cannot take tools pays one refused request before the loop retries without.

**The domain proposal tools** (`server/src/services/domainProposals.ts`; P2-B for the first three, P2-C for the fourth): the first native tools **granted by the conversation, not by a family**. They exist in exactly one place — the conversation Maurice opened at night to propose domains, while a proposal in it is still open or an adopted domain's notes still wait for the member's word — whatever the families or the experimental flag say; `domainToolsFor(conversationId, memberId)` answers the four or nothing, and every call re-checks on the conversation itself. Appended after the MCP roster so an ordinary conversation's cached prefix does not move. **`domains__propose`** — `list` the open proposals; `show` one with every conversation (up to 200, with ids); `add` a domain the member named. **`domains__adjust`** — `rename`, `merge` (sources `superseded`), `split` by conversation ids, `dismiss` (its conversations never come up again). **`domains__adopt`** — the only thing that creates a domain from a proposal, after the member's explicit yes: a row of `maurices` (kind `domain`, the summary as its statement, the three closest conversations baked into its context), every conversation of the proposal bound to it, the first brief started in the background under the `system` spender; answers *no note written* so Maurice offers the seeding. **`domains__seed`** — the garden seeded on consent, only after the member said yes to the notes themselves; `action: "decline"` records the no. Mechanics in *Domains and their briefs*.

**The corpus's mapping tool — `corpus__map_conversations`** (`tools/corpus/src/mcp_server.py`, the arithmetic in `src/domain_map.py`): groups the caller's conversations by their vectors — one centroid per conversation, spherical k-means, a merge of close centroids, a second level on any group above `split_above` (200) — and returns groups of conversation ids with their cohesion, no names, no dates. Reads the member's file only; `tools/corpus/scripts/map_domains.py` calls the same code.

**Public vs. private** is enforced by `.gitignore` and the container's dockerignore: public `garden`, `corpus`, `shared`, `mcp_gateway`; everything else is the `maurice-tools` overlay. Secrets in `.env`, never in tracked config.

**`mail`** (private overlay, September 2026): reads Proton (via Bridge) and iCloud mailboxes; the only write is `triage(account, uid, action)` over a closed vocabulary in the config (one state and at most one topic per message); no send, no delete, no arbitrary move; bodies returned stripped and wrapped as untrusted; deterministic `[rules]` run before the model (46% of the inbox); a nightly launchd proposal (`com.maurice.mail-proposal`) classified by local `qwen3.6:35b-a3b` with reasoning off; an audit log at `~/.maurice/mail-audit.jsonl` keyed on Message-ID; reading horizons per state. Its config is one person's method and gates sharing the code.

**Gaps**: the gateway itself enforces neither families nor the experimental flag (a direct MCP client gets the full roster; the native tools are the exception by construction, but `corpus__map_conversations` is mounted like any corpus tool); `web` and `signals` cannot be turned off; the parent `garden` prefix opens all sub-families; no per-tool sandboxing; discovery is positional; résonances and flashcards have no MCP face; the garden tool knows the unreviewed mark (`list_notes` answers `unreviewed: true`, `update_note` with a body clears it, `create_note` never sets it); Homebrew's `python@3.14` needs `scripts/fix-python-expat.sh` after every upgrade; `tools/calendar` shadows the stdlib module.

## The web garden

`web/` is the Astro engine. **Themes** live under `web/themes/<name>/`, resolved per request in `web/src/middleware.ts`: `?theme=` (wins, sets a year-long dated cookie) → that cookie, *while it is newer than the owner's choice* → `X-Maurice-Theme` (the app's picker, stored in `garden_settings.web_theme`, its date sent as `X-Maurice-Theme-Since`) → `THEME` env → `manuscript`. Shipped garden themes: manuscript, botanical, newsprint, terminal (`default` is the hidden base). Collections: notes, blog, essays, pages, resources (books, articles, movies, series, podcasts, games, people — the cards), fiches (`<slug>-fiche.md`, private garden only). `public` flag = published; `encrypted` notes are encrypted in the built HTML (AES-256-GCM, `PRIVATE_CONTENT_PASSWORD`). Wiki-links `[[slug]]` and typed refs `[text](note:slug)` become links (`remark-cross-ref.mjs`); MOC notes render their children as cards.

**The unreviewed banner** (19 September 2026): a note Maurice wrote at a domain's seeding and the owner has not reviewed (`meta.opened: false`) opens on a banner — *Written by Maurice, not reviewed yet · From N conversations, with <model>* — with, for the owner, **Keep** (`POST /api/v1/garden-tools/review-note`; `clearUnreviewed` drops the one line and commits) and **Throw away** (the toolbar's delete). The notes index and a MOC's child list show *to review* beside such a note; both live in the default theme's `NoteDetail` and `NotesList`, inherited by every shipped theme.

**One built engine per household** (since 14 September 2026): `web/dist/server/entry.mjs`, started by `scripts/start-web.sh` on :4321, loopback only; the Bun proxy sends `X-Maurice-Garden`, `X-Maurice-Base`, `X-Maurice-Theme`, `X-Maurice-Theme-Since`, `X-Maurice-Owner`, `X-Maurice-Shared` (all stripped from incoming requests first) and the engine reads them into an `AsyncLocalStorage` (`src/lib/garden-context.ts`). Every collection is read off disk per request (`web/src/lib/content-fs.ts`, `notes-fs.ts`); ~127 MB for a four-member household. Live reload streams from `GET /api/v1/garden-tools/events`. Images are served by the server off disk at `/images/<member>/**` (own garden entirely, another member's only under `resources/`); `/api/images/<name>` is what Maurice writes into a note body, unauthenticated. The remote-cover sweep is the server's (`services/gardenImages.ts`).

**The owner's toolbar** (flip public/private, delete, drag-reorder a MOC, open in Obsidian or Working Copy, translate, share to social, and the review gestures) calls `POST /api/v1/garden-tools/*`, always on the caller's own garden; writes go through `atomicWrite` + `autoCommit`. `translate` and `social-publish` need the source checkout and answer 501 in the container. **Owner mode** is `Astro.locals.owner` from the proxy header (`GARDEN_OWNER=1` for a bare `astro dev`). The public site publishes to Cloudflare Pages via `scripts/publish-web.sh`; a Pages function adds `?t=YYYY-MM-DD` time travel from an R2 bucket. **Tests**: `web/e2e/` (Playwright, `npm run e2e`, 73 green); `docs/garden-server-mode.md` records the chantier.

**Gaps**: the private overlay `maurice-web/` is symlinked into `web/src` (use `grep -R`); the activity indicator is transient; the search index is single-locale. Per-garden theme wiring is joined (dated 19 September 2026): a reader's `?theme=` cookie holds only until the owner picks again.

## From specialized Maurices to domains

Until 19 September 2026 a row of `maurices` was a **persona**: a named assistant with its own prompt, model, creativity, a frozen bundle of context and a **hat** chosen from a grid; **Maurice Studio** picked one to summon and the send button's picker switched specialist. That afternoon the design changed: **there is one Maurice**, and what a member used to shape as a second Maurice is a **domain**. The table kept its shape (`services/maurices.ts`, `routes/maurices.ts`) and gained one column, **`kind`**: `domain` (the default; NULL reads as such) — a part of its creator's life, with a brief — or `companion` — a **reading companion**, one book in bound context followed at the reading position with a conduct prompt, no brief. `hat` and `palette` remain as columns, but nothing writes or reads them: the apps draw a mark on the member's accent instead (a closed book for a domain, open pages for a companion). The migration is additive and reversible.

**The one-time sort** (`migrateMauriceKinds()` in `db.ts`): at the first start on a database that predates the column, a row whose bound context is exactly one book on the `progress` scope becomes a companion, anything else a domain; at home *JTTW guide* → companion, *Yi Jing* → domain. The sort never revisits a row; the member re-sorts in the editor or with `PATCH /api/maurices/:id { kind }`. Ownership, `maurice_access` (how a guest gets what an admin grants), the model preference and the tool families are unchanged; `POST /api/conversations { maurice_id }` still binds a conversation to a row. The **everyday Maurice** is the null row: no bound context, the member's `everyday_model`, the household's `everyday_thinking`.

**In the app** (Mac and iOS): the Studio (`StudioViews.swift`), the summon picker, the hat badge and the hat/colour grids are gone; the send button is Maurice's boater on the member's accent, the header shows one Maurice. In their place the **domains list** (`DomainsViews.swift`, a button beside *New conversation*), the **domain editor** (`DomainEditorView.swift`: name, tagline, Domain / Reading companion, *what it is about*, context, model, creativity, reasoning, tools) and the greeting `DomainGreeting` of a bound conversation. `Hats.swift` lives on in the loading animation. **Rooms**: unchanged — Maurice answers when summoned, a room bound at creation still reads that row's prompt and context, and a room **never** gets a brief; nothing in the apps binds a room to a domain any more (`PATCH /api/conversations/:id/maurice` remains for a client that wants to).

**Maurice Maurice is gone** (the evening of 19 September 2026, after one day of existence). The built-in specialist who answered questions about Maurice from these notes — not a row, defined in code, heading every member's list, the whole documentation baked into every turn — was replaced by the **documentation tool** `maurice_docs` that the everyday Maurice calls in any conversation (see *The MCP tool ecosystem*). What went with him: `builtinMaurice()`, `builtinMauriceModel()` (its model choice lives on as `docsModel()`), the `builtin` flag, the *About Maurice* section of the domains list, the locked model pill and the boater mark; the admin card is now titled *Maurice's documentation*. Conversations bound to his id were unbound once at start; a client that still sends `maurice_id: "maurice-maurice"` gets 404.

**Ships vs. exists**: the column, the sort, the routes, the list, the editor, the documentation tool are in the server and the Maurice app and ship; the hosted households run the same sort at first start. Carnet never showed a hat; since the evening of the 19th it reads `/api/domains` and writes `/api/maurices` only to follow a book (`kind: companion`); it has no chat, so the documentation tool does not reach it.

**Gaps**: *New domain* in the editor is the hand-made path that remains beside the night's proposals; a companion's pinned conversation is found (the most recently touched bound conversation, named by `GET /api/domains`), not designed from the book's page; the documentation is English only.

## Domains and their briefs

**What a domain is today**: a row of `maurices` of kind `domain` (see the previous section); a `companion` row is not a domain — no brief, not in the prompt, not on the brief routes. A domain shared with a guest is the creator's, not the guest's (`domainsOf` in `services/domainBriefs.ts`, `isDomain` in `services/maurices.ts`). **The brief**: two or three paragraphs Maurice keeps on the domain, his working memory made visible — one `domain_briefs` row per domain and member.

**Written at night** (04:00 local, an hour after the corpus reconciles) or on demand. For every domain of every non-guest member the server gathers what touched it since the last brief — the conversations bound to it, then the corpus's semantic search on name, tagline and the prompt's opening (`corpus__search`, `source_type: conversation`, scoped to the member), then the full-text search on the name; never a conversation bound to another domain, nor one the member said nothing in since; **eight at most**, bound ones first, each an excerpt of ~1 800 characters. A conversation imported since the last brief carries the dates of its export, older than `read_until`, so `conversations.imported_at` is compared with the brief's `updated_at` and the conversation is read whole, once. With nothing new no model is called. Otherwise the `domain_brief` invocation rewrites from the previous brief and the excerpts: ~300 words for a first brief, ~200 for a rewrite, in the member's language, second person, no title, no list, nothing lent to the member they did not say, no question; a reply that hit `max_tokens` is a failure and the previous brief stays; `read_until` moves to the newest message read. A corrected brief is handed to the next rewrite as *the member's* words. Every call is charged to the `system` spender under the night's cap; the run stops at the first domain refused.

**Read in every private conversation — as an index, since 20 September 2026.** The prompt carries *{name}'s domains*: **one line per domain**, its name and a sentence, newest rewrite first, under 1 200 estimated tokens (`briefsSection`, `INDEX_BUDGET_TOKENS`); the brief itself is loaded only when a question falls into a domain, through the native **`domain_brief`** tool (`services/domainTools.ts`, the name the index showed, forgiving match, scoped to the member). A domain with no brief is still listed. The morning of the 20th the budget had gone 3 000 → 5 000 to fit every brief whole; the evening's measurement undid that: eleven briefs weigh **4 903 tokens** and rode entire into a conversation about a family holiday in the Galápagos where none applied. The same eleven now come to **875 tokens**. The one-liner is written by the night in a **second call** after the brief (a second output format would spoil a tuned prompt), fails softly to the brief's opening sentences, and is **dropped when the member rewrites their brief by hand**; `scripts/backfill-brief-summaries.ts` fills in the ones that predate the change. Two consequences: the brief now arrives as a **tool result**, so a night's rewrite no longer moves every conversation's cached prefix; and the tool is offered **only where the index is carried** — never in a room, never for another member, one flag in `services/claude.ts` holding both rules together.

**Routes** (`routes/domains.ts`). `GET /api/domains` lists the member's domains and companions — own rows, and the rows granted to a guest (`mine: false`, no brief) — each domain with `brief: { updated_at, model, sources }` or `null` and `notes: { total, unreviewed, web_path }`, each companion with `book_id` and the `conversation_id` of its pinned conversation. Creator only, 404 otherwise and for a companion: `GET /api/domains/:id/brief`, `PUT` with `{ text }` (`model = member`; empty erases), `DELETE`, `POST …/brief/refresh` (`outcome` ∈ `written | unchanged | failed | capped`, 429 when the night's allowance is spent). The console's "domain briefs" card has "Rewrite now" (`POST /admin/briefs/run`).

**How a domain is proposed** (P2-B, `services/domainMapping.ts`, 05:00 local; the proposals and tools in `services/domainProposals.ts`). For every member the night looks at the conversations attached to no domain: their own (not a room), opened by them, bound to no row, not spoken for by a proposal, with at least one turn of theirs. Nothing is looked at under six of them; an open proposal makes the member wait; one older than forty-two days is put away as `expired`. Then the guard of the opened conversation — never a child, never a guest, never twice within the household's days — before a cent is spent. The corpus groups the conversations by their vectors (`corpus__map_conversations`, one centroid per conversation on the member's own turns, k-means on the sphere, a second level on any group above 200); the server keeps, on a corpus of 200 conversations or more, the groups of eight over four months (alive under 180 days, *lived* beyond), on a smaller one those of three over two months (alive under a year). **Maturity before spending**: fewer than two alive groups and the night writes and calls nothing. Otherwise the `domain_mapping` invocation names and describes each group, up to eighteen a night, from twenty titles and seven openings, in the member's language (their locale, else the household's, else English), answering `is_domain` and a `split_hint`; a group it calls several things is cut by the model itself when small enough to see every title (eighty at most) and left aside otherwise. What remains, when at least two alive domains are among it, is written as `domain_proposals`: the three alive ones that count now are *presented*; the model writes the opening message (150–250 words) and the conversation is opened through `openConversation`, the proposals tied to it, the four `domains__*` tools granted to it alone. Every call is checked against the night's cap before and charged to `system` after. The console's "domain mapping" card shows one line per member with its outcome (opened, waiting, guarded, too few, not mature, capped, failed) and "Map now" (`POST /admin/mapping/run`); `POST /api/admin/domains/map` does one member (`dry_run` names but writes and opens nothing; `force` walks past the guard); `GET /api/admin/domains/proposals` lists what was proposed. First real run on a copy of home: Paola's 21 conversations gave 3 proposals in 39 s for 1.2 ¢; her merge-adopt-dismiss turn created *Ma santé* and wrote its first brief for another 1.1 ¢.

**In that conversation** Maurice holds the four tools (see *The MCP tool ecosystem*): propose, adjust, adopt, seed. The prompt section names the proposals and the rule: nothing becomes a domain without the member's explicit yes, and their words on what a domain is about are right by definition. **Adopting** creates the row, binds the conversations and starts the first brief in the background; the domain then appears in the app's list with its brief.

**Seeding the garden** (P2-C, `services/domainSeeding.ts`): **a yes to the domain is not a yes to the notes.** After an adoption Maurice offers, once, to write a few notes; only when the member agrees to the notes themselves does he call `domains__seed`. One call to the night model (`domain_seed` invocation) on the sixteen most recent conversations bound to the domain returns a JSON the server turns into **one hub note** (a MOC: *what I understood*, *the open threads*, the topic notes as wiki-links, *where it comes from*) and **up to three topic notes** under it (`parent: <hub>`). In the member's language, under `notes/<locale>/`, slug from the title (`-2` on a collision); one git commit for the lot, pushed when the garden has a remote. Every note carries `meta.opened: false`, `meta.author: maurice`, `domain`, `domain_name`, `role` (`hub` | `topic`), `model`, `written_at`, `sources`, and a *Where it comes from* section at the end of the body. Charged to the member; 25–40 seconds, under one cent on the owner's corpus. **Reviewing**: *Keep* (the web banner, `POST /api/v1/garden-tools/review-note`), correct (`update_note` with a body clears the mark), or throw away (delete). The app's garden list shows *to review*; the domain's page says *N notes in the garden · M to review* with a way to the hub. Absence of the mark is the reviewed state.

**Bringing a history in** (P4): the Maurice app's settings carry **Import my conversations** (`ImportConversationsView.swift`; one card per provider, the button, the live progress) — a setting, deliberately not a step of the onboarding. `POST /api/import?provider=anthropic|chatgpt` (multipart `file`), `GET /api/import/status?job=`, `GET /api/import/history?provider=` (`routes/import.ts`, `services/chatImport.ts`, shared with the console's `/admin/users/:id/import*`); guests refused. The corpus's `import_chat_export` (`tools/corpus/src/chat_import.py`) writes each thread as a conversation of the member's with the dates of its export, its `origin` and `imported_at`, indexed, bound to no domain — so the very next night maps it, and the briefs read it whole once. The everyday prompt may mention the import a single time to a member who never did it (`importHintSection`).

**The demo household**: `server/scripts/seed-demo.ts` writes Théo's history (`server/scripts/demo-conversations.ts`, CC0 — bread, the balcony garden, Japanese, the bike that stopped, mostly badged as imported); `scripts/seed-demo-household.sh` seeds and starts a server on :3004 (`--seed`, `--archive`, `--morning` runs the corpus reconcile and the mapping at once so Maurice opens the proposal conversation on the spot — tried on 19 September for 1.4 ¢).

**The domain page in the app** (Mac and iOS, `DomainBriefView.swift`): when the brief was last rewritten and by whom (*In your words* once corrected), the text in an editor, the notes count, and the actions — **Save**, **Rewrite now**, **Talk about it** (a new conversation bound to the domain, its baked-in context preloaded), **Erase**, **Edit** (the domain editor). A companion's row resumes its pinned conversation or starts one; a domain granted to a guest opens a conversation on it, no page.

**Ships vs. exists**: the briefs, the domains, the proposals, the seeding and the import are in the server, the web garden and the Maurice app; nothing depends on the private tools — the corpus ships and the mapping's arithmetic is in it; a hosted household maps on its Scaleway-embedded store the same way. Carnet has its own Domains shelf (see *Carnet*).

**Gaps**: seeding is offered in the proposal conversation only (`seedDomain` takes any domain, nothing calls it outside the tool; a *Write notes* button on the page is the natural next step); the seeded notes carry the personal detail of the conversations — the mark asks the member to read first; a seeded note reaches the corpus only when the gateway's watcher sees it; the app shows nothing of a proposal but the conversation; a big grab-bag is left aside (the owner's block of conversations lived with Maurice stays out until cut finer); the model's cut is not deterministic; a member's language is guessed when never set; a companion's pinned conversation is found, not designed from the book's page; the briefs are the creator's (a shared brief for a shared domain is open); the budget is estimated and a correction costs one cache miss; a one-word domain still pulls in neighbours; an imported conversation is read whole once by the timestamp of the import, and a very large import can crowd one night; the demo's eight personas became eight domains of Théo's — the seed is to be trimmed.

## The chat experience

Native SwiftUI (`app/Maurice/Views/ChatView.swift`, streaming in `Services/ChatService.swift`). The sidebar's magnifier opens a full-text search over the member's rooms (`GET /api/conversations/search`). The header is floating glass; "…" opens a details sheet (title editable via `PATCH /api/conversations/:id`, Maurice, model, dates, people, room actions; with the cost meter on, a **Cost and tokens** section computed client-side by `ConversationUsage` from the usage persisted on each assistant message). In a private conversation Maurice reads the member's domain briefs before they type; never in a room.

**A conversation Maurice opens** (19 September 2026): the server creates it for one member with a first message in his voice (`conversations.opened_by = 'maurice'`, `services/openedConversations.ts`), titled, unread; the member's channel gets `conversation_opened`, a member with no socket live an APNs push ("Maurice: …"); the sidebar row carries the caption *Opened by Maurice* and the unread dot; opening marks it read (`POST /api/conversations/:id/read`), replying is an ordinary turn on the member's own model. The history would start with an assistant turn, which the Messages API refuses, so a constant user lead is placed before it for every provider. Who may receive one: never a child, never a guest, never twice within the household's days. Two callers: the operator — `POST /api/admin/conversations/open` with `{ username | member_id, text, title?, maurice_id?, force?, dry_run? }` or `scripts/open-conversation.sh <username> "<text>"`, no model called — and **the night that proposes domains**, whose first message the night model writes and whose conversation alone holds the `domains__*` tools. Carnet lists and reads these conversations on its Domains shelf; it cannot answer.

**Streaming**: a variable-speed reveal (~100–300 chars/s, `StreamingRow`) and respectful auto-scroll; deleting a thread stops its stream first. **A reply survives the cut** (19 September 2026): if the connection is lost the activity line says "Connexion perdue, Maurice continue…" and the app re-attaches with `GET /api/conversations/:id/turn`; foregrounding re-attaches to a turn started in the last five minutes. **Stop** (⏹) tells the server first (`POST …/turn/stop`), then cancels locally. **One reply at a time**: a `409` removes the refused message and re-attaches. **Data cards** (`DataCardStack`) render `tool_data` rows deterministically, titled `"{tool} · {n} rows"`, capped at 50 — the floor under tool-result hallucination. **Math**: `\(…\)`, `\[…\]`, `$$…$$` rendered with SwiftMath, skipping code. Markdown via MarkdownUI; Copy and Regenerate (last turn) on each answer; photos from camera, library or clipboard, downsized to the vision tier's ceiling. **Dictation**: on-device speech; the iPhone Action Button opens a fresh thread and listens (`DictateIntent`). **Model switcher**: the composer's pill, grouped by provider, limited to configured providers. **Cost meter**: off by default, a coin under each reply with the cost and cached share, a popover with the token split, rounds and model; token counts, never "$0.00", for an unpriced model. **Liquid Glass** on iOS/macOS 26 (`Views/Glass.swift`); targets iOS 17 / macOS 14.

**Source cards** (20 September 2026): a corpus or web search renders under the reply as a **line of pills** — four overlapping 20-point thumbnails and a count, the height of a line of text — which opens a **drawer** holding every source at a readable size: cover when the garden has one, title, where it came from, the passage that matched; a web source is a link, a garden one is not. One payload for both searches (`card: "sources"`, `services/sourceCards.ts`, drawn by `SourcesCard.swift`). Before it, a corpus search was a folded key/value dump and a web search rendered *nothing at all* — its result never became a data block, so twenty-seven pages read left no trace but what the model retyped. The cover is read from the frontmatter first (only the local `image` of cards and article fiches resolves; `thumbnail`, `poster_path` and `image_filename` do not), the conventional `<collection>/<locale>-<slug>.jpg` second, and the file is stat-ed either way; the URL handed over is the open twin `/api/garden-images/…`, since an `AsyncImage` sends no credentials. Sources are de-duplicated by conversation or file — one Guardian article came back four times — and the count reported is of distinct sources.

**Gaps**: image generation has no progress; files reach a chat only through the omnibox; regenerate is single-step; **no web chat client** (the web is the garden); no cross-conversation cost view; the details sheet does not say who opened a conversation (only the sidebar caption does); the user lead before Maurice's opener is one English sentence the model reads as the member's turn; the Maurice app has no URL scheme yet for Carnet's *Reply in Maurice*.

## The context composer

The app's context tray (`Views/ComposerViews.swift`) over `server/src/services/composer/*` and `routes/composer.ts`. Loadable: **notes** (with descendants following `[[wiki-links]]` breadth-first; `maurice-docs` with descendants is the whole system documentation), **books** (chapter scope `all` / `up_to` / pick / `progress`; representation `summary` or `full`), **fiches** (with fragments), **past conversations** (summary past 8k tokens, cached against a transcript hash in `conversation_summaries`; `representation: full` pins the transcript), **files**, **folders**. The **omnibox** `GET /api/v1/composer/search` searches all of them, ordered by `updated_at` by default. The tray shows a running total against a **200,000-token budget** (`CTX_BUDGET` in `composer/weights.ts`); binaries carry no estimate and ride as content blocks. Two layers: **baked-in** (a row's `context_json`, locked) and **added** (removable); a third rides beside them, invisible in the tray — the member's domain briefs, placed after the loaded context, edited on the domain page. **Frozen snapshots** (`composer/specs.ts`): the resolved set is frozen at save, the content stays live; refresh to regrow. A book on **`progress`** follows `reading_progress` (Carnet's reader writes it), loads summaries up to the reader's chapter, recomputes each turn, can be **paused** or **reset**; the block names the book and its calibre `book_id`, chapters numbered 1-based over body chapters (`classifyChapters` = the tool's `_body_chapters`); "I've read three more chapters" calls `calibre__set_reading_progress`.

**Ships**: notes, conversations, files, fiches (`composer/fiches.ts`). **Books** need the experimental Calibre tool. **Gaps**: room context is shared; the budget is a fixed 200k, not the model's window, and loaded context is never trimmed to fit.

## Carnet

The second native client (own repo, iOS 17+, XcodeGen, SwiftUI), on the **Grid** theme with Liquid Glass chrome on iOS 26 (`CarnetApp/Views/Glass.swift`). Three roles: **Log** (meals, exercise, breathwork, notes, places, documents, tasks — radial FAB, free-text parsing, share extension, passive location; backed by `signals`, `uploads`, `places`), **Dashboards** (server-defined `layouts`), **Content** (reading, garden entries, résonances, and what Maurice keeps on the member). **Reading**: the Library lists Calibre books with covers, chapters, summaries and position (synced per member; `enabled = 0` pauses); highlights with note and colour; offline downloads; the Articles shelf lists what was saved by share sheet or clipper, with lead image (`/api/garden-images/<member>/…`), summary, highlights, notes, and *not a fiche yet* until written on.

**Domains** (19 September 2026, P3-C): a third shelf beside Books and Articles, listing the conversations Maurice opened (*Opened by Maurice*, *Unread* until opened), the member's domains (brief age and hand; a guest sees *Shared with you* and no page) and their reading companions (each leading to its book). A domain's page (`Views/Domains/DomainBriefView.swift`): the brief in an editor, **Save** (`PUT /api/domains/:id/brief`), **Rewrite now** (`POST …/brief/refresh`), **Erase** (`DELETE`); no *Talk about it* and no *Edit* — Carnet has no chat, and the domain itself is edited in the Maurice app. A conversation Maurice opened is read (`Views/Domains/OpenedConversationView.swift`) and marked read with `POST /api/conversations/:id/read`; *Reply in Maurice* opens `maurice://conversations/<id>` when a build of the Maurice app answers that scheme. A book's *This book* drawer says whether Maurice follows it and offers **Follow**: `POST /api/maurices` with `kind: companion`, the book on the composer's `progress` scope. The API is `Services/DomainsAPI.swift`. **Push**: Carnet keeps no socket; once signed in it hands the device token to every paired household as `POST /api/users/me/device-token` with `platform: carnet-ios` and the local household id as `household_tag` (`Services/CarnetNotifications.swift`); a tap switches to the household it came from and opens the conversation. The server picks the APNs topic by platform (`carnet-ios` → `APNS_CARNET_TOPIC`, default `eu.chezmaurice.carnet`; anything else → `APNS_TOPIC`).

The **share extension** posts a URL, file or data as the active member, sending the Safari page's rendered DOM, with an offline queue. The **Garden** section lists every entry across collections, ordered by last written; **Résonance** files a dated `[[wiki-link]]` block on another entry's fiche. Two compile-time scopes (`CarnetScope.current`): **Full** and **Minimal** (reading only, Domains shelf included — the TestFlight cut since 11 September 2026; a device joins with an invite code then a PIN). `./build-testflight.sh` uploads; version `1.0.0` from `./VERSION`.

**Gaps**: no chat (reads and marks read, cannot answer; the Maurice app has no URL scheme yet); a companion's conversation is not opened from Carnet; English only (the briefs are in the member's language, the chrome is not); flashcards are server-side only; no unified navigation across roles; reading is iOS-only; the icon is a flat PNG; Reduce Transparency untested. The Carnet note still says the push is refused (`DeviceTokenNotForTopic`); the households note and the code say the topic is chosen by platform since the evening of the 19th — the Carnet note is the stale one.

## Shared gardens

Sharing is a fact about a **note**; gardens are **derived** (`services/gardens.ts`, `routes/gardens.ts`). A note's audience = owner + `note_shares` rows; `audienceKey()` sorts and joins member ids; a garden is the set of notes with the same key, computed on the fly (`gardensFor(memberId)`); `garden_settings.web_theme` per audience. Verbs: `GET /api/v1/gardens`; `GET /api/v1/gardens/note/:owner/:slug/access`; `POST …/share`; `POST …/leave` (remove yourself); `PATCH /api/v1/gardens/:id` (theme). The app's sidebar lists the gardens you tend, newest-edit first. On the web each member's garden is `/g/<member>` per `web/gardens/gardens.json`.

**Gaps**: access is grant-and-leave, no read-only vs. co-editor, no audit; "earned by presence" (joining a garden by participating in a room that loads it) is not implemented. The note's "theme wiring is a seam" gap is closed since 19 September 2026 (see *The web garden*).

## The files library

Per-member folders and files (`FilesLibraryView` in `SettingsView.swift`; `services/files.ts`, `routes/files.ts`). Nestable folders by `parent_id`; create, upload (iOS file importer or camera; macOS drag-and-drop), rename, delete (cascades, unlinks bytes); **5 GB quota** per member. Kinds by extension: text (`.md`, `.txt`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.yaml`, `.log`, `.rtf`), image, PDF, generic. Bytes at `{dataDir}/files/{id}{ext}`, served raw at `/api/files/{id}/raw`. Attaching goes through the composer: text becomes prompt text, binaries become content blocks (no token estimate, de-duplicated by id). **Gaps**: browsing lives in Settings; no iOS drag-and-drop; no previews or full-text search of file contents.

## Households, rooms & devices

**A server is one household; a device can hold several; switching member is a tap and a PIN.** First run (`PairingView.swift`): enter a server URL, `GET /api/health` confirms it and returns the household's identity; `SessionStore.swift` persists paired households with one session token each; `switchHousehold()` flips the active one. `UserPickerView.swift`: PIN login (`POST /api/auth/login`), admin login, or an invite code (`POST /api/auth/enroll`, then `/api/auth/set-pin`; guests need no PIN). Invite codes: 8 characters, one active per member, ~7 days. **Roles**: admin, standard, guest — a guest's reach is `guest_contacts`, checked both directions (`guestCanReach()`), plus the domains granted (`maurice_access`). Orthogonal to the role, a member can be marked a **child** (`users.is_child`, a box on the console's member page): Maurice never opens a conversation for a child, and the night proposes nothing to them; it changes nothing else. **Rooms**: a 1:1 is a room with one human; `addParticipant()` enforces guest reach; in a room 💬 (human-only) is the default button and Return posts it, ➤ summons Maurice with ⌘Return; read state per participant; the foyer's unread roll-up also counts a conversation Maurice opened that you have not opened yet. The per-user channel (`/api/me/ws`) carries `activity`, `conversation_added` and `conversation_opened`. **A conversation Maurice opens** is guarded by the household: never a child, never a guest, never twice within `households.maurice_opens_min_days` (fifteen by default, "Days between two conversations Maurice opens" in the console); the operator's route can pass `force`, the night never will. **Moderation**: long-press → Report or Block in shared rooms only; reports at `/api/reports`, child-safety first. **Push**: APNs (`push.ts`, `apns.ts`) for a room's activity, an invitation, or a conversation Maurice opened ("Maurice: …"); tokens carry `platform` and `household_tag`; the platform picks the topic (`carnet-ios` → `APNS_CARNET_TOPIC`, else `APNS_TOPIC`); dead tokens are pruned on Apple's say-so.

**The household archive** (since 19 September 2026): a versioned `.maurice.tar.gz` (`maurice-archive` v1, `services/archive.ts`, format in `docs/household-archive.md`) holding everything the household *is* — the databases as `VACUUM INTO` snapshots, the gardens with their git history and `gardens.json`, images, files, uploads, avatars, `config.toml`, a `manifest.json`. Left out: backups, logs, the corpus vector index (regenerable), `.env`, the bare remotes under `~/.maurice/git`. It contains the provider keys: **it is a secret**. Export: the console's "Export this household" (`GET /admin/export`) or a bearer token on `GET /api/admin/export`, streamed. Import into an empty data directory: `scripts/import-household.sh <archive> [dir]` on a Mac, `scripts/container.sh import <archive>` for a local container, `ops/household.sh add <host> <name> <domain> --from <archive>` on a shared host (over ssh's stdin, no copy stays). Still by hand after: the corpus index and the gardens' `origin` remotes.

**Gaps**: single-tenant server; the `/api/auth/pair` device ceremony is implemented but unused; sessions don't expire; a guest's access to domains has no override; "child" is a box, not a birth date.

## Knowledge capabilities

**Garden** (`tools/garden/server.py`, ships): full CRUD over the Markdown garden, always on request. Notes (`create_note`, `update_note`, `get_note`, `list_notes`, `delete_note`, `set_image`, `toggle_public` / `toggle_private`); journal (`write_daily_note`, `get_daily_note`, `search_daily_notes`, `write_dream`); fragments (`append_fragment`); resources (`search_*` then `create_*_entry`; metadata and covers from Google Books, TMDB, IGDB, Podcast Index, Wikidata, Open Graph); fiches (`open_fiche`, `update_fiche`, `promote_fiche` — a card is the reader's verdict); imagery and publishing (`generate_evocation`, `generate_hero_image` via fal.ai; `publish_content`, `deploy_site`); people (`link_contact_to_person`, `find_person_by_contact`). Storage: git-backed Markdown under `~/.maurice/gardens/<username>/`, openable in Obsidian or Working Copy.

**A media entry has faces**: the **card** (`<slug>.md`, published), the **fiche** (`<slug>-fiche.md`: `meta:`, `## Commentaire`, `## Résonances`, a `_fragments/` directory), the **flashcards** (`<slug>-fiche/_cards/`). A fiche is *opened* by a deliberate gesture; an article share writes one with `meta.opened: false` until the reader writes on it. **The same mark on a note Maurice wrote** (19 September 2026): a note seeded on a domain carries `meta.opened: false` and `meta.author: maurice` — the one exception to "Maurice writes only when asked", and it is asked, twice; the mark goes when the member keeps, corrects or throws it away (see *Domains and their briefs*). `list_notes` says `unreviewed: true`; the app's garden list shows *to review*.

**Articles pipeline** (ships): `POST /api/v1/garden/articles`, called by Carnet's share sheet, the clipper and the garden tool; accepts the browser's rendered DOM; de-duplicates under `rel=canonical`; a site refusing robots yields a **bookmark** fiche (`status: needs-capture`) completed by a later browser share; AI summary generated after the response. **Highlights & résonances** (ship with Carnet): highlights in SQLite per member; a résonance is a dated block under `## Résonances` on the target's fiche with a literal `[[wiki-link]]` — no link table, the markdown is the truth.

**Flashcards** (ship, September 2026): generated on demand from a chapter, a book, a fiche or a fragment, highlights covered first; one file per pass in the **Obsidian Spaced Repetition** syntax with the plugin's `<!--SR:…-->` schedule; frontmatter holds the source hash and a fingerprint per card (hand-edited cards survive regeneration); `_cards/` is git-ignored; French by default, `cards_lang` on the fiche, a *vocabulary* mode; `/api/v1/garden/cards`.

**Corpus** (`tools/corpus`, ships): RAG over reading and conversations — `search`, `search_by_tags`, `search_by_author`, `search_in_book`, `search_book_summaries`, `get_chunk_context`, `index_conversation` (one, or every one in the background), `reconcile_status`, `prune` (with `shared: true` for the pool), `map_conversations` (the domains' grouping), `import_chat_export`; indexes notes, fiches, fragments and cards as they change (the server pushes writes; a watcher in the gateway catches the rest); vector store **sqlite-vec**, one file per member under `tools/corpus/data/vectors/` (`<member>.db`, `_default.db` for shared content). **Embedding model since 19 September 2026: the Qwen3-Embedding family at 1024 dimensions** (`nomic-embed-text` before it put every French conversation in one block): **0.6B through Ollama at home, `qwen3-embedding-8b` on Scaleway for a hosted instance** — matryoshka-trained, so every size is asked for the same `dimensions`. `corpus_meta` pins the model and width that wrote a file and the store **refuses to open** under another (`sqlite_vec_store.py`, `_check_pin`); a model change is a full re-index into a fresh store (`python -m src.main index --force`, then `--source conversations`; a night for the home corpus), the old store kept beside it. Config is `vector_size` plus optional `query_prefix` / `document_prefix`. Hosted households installed before this keep `bge-multilingual-gemma2` at 3584. **Live conversations**: the after-reply push (`indexConversationInBackground`) had called the tool by its bare name and the gateway's "Unknown tool" came back as text, so no conversation since the June import had entered the index; the call now goes through `corpusCall`, and the nightly reconcile is the safety net.

**Bringing your history** (P4): `import_chat_export` ingests the official Claude and ChatGPT export `.zip`s; the member does it themselves from the Maurice app's settings through `/api/import` (`routes/import.ts`, `services/chatImport.ts`), and each imported conversation carries `conversations.imported_at` (`chat_import.py`) for the two nights — see *Domains and their briefs*.

**Tracks** (`tools/tracks`, experimental): `request_deep_research`, `get_research_status`, `generate_briefing`, `get_latest_briefing`, `get_dossier`, `get_dossier_tree`, `list_dossiers`, `trigger_signal_report`; SQLite storage; findings can be published to the garden.

**An order of search** (20 September 2026). The corpus ranks nothing across sources — cosine score, every source mixed — and conversations outnumber the garden sixty to one in the owner's index (88 726 chunks against 1 408 notes): unfiltered, *"Où en suis-je dans ma pratique du violon ?"* returns four conversations and not one note, while a garden note answers it. The everyday prompt therefore asks each layer separately, one call per layer **in the same round** (`corpusNotice` in `services/claude.ts`): **the garden** (`note`, `fiche`, `card`, `fragment`) — what the member decided was worth keeping, which is the promise Maurice makes them — **then what they have said** (`conversation`), **then what they only read** (`book`, `dossier`, `thought`). The first draft said *one search per step*; the model ignored the sequence and fired both searches at once, which is better on both counts, so the instruction was rewritten to say what the model did, the order now weighing the results rather than sequencing the calls. `corpus__search`'s own description listed neither `conversation` nor `card` among its `source_type` values, while conversations are the majority of the index: corrected.

**Narrowed before the model reads it** (`services/corpusResults.ts`, same evening). One search weighed **3 233 tokens, 1 766 of them bookkeeping**, and three searches in a turn came to some 15 500 against a 5 655-token prompt. Four cuts: the turn's **own conversation** is dropped (it came back as its own top two hits); hits are **grouped by source**, best passage kept (six of ten were another passage of a document already listed); what remains is held to a **floor of 0.35 and to 0.15 below the best hit**, since the corpus has no threshold and answers *"Galápagos"* with the ten least distant things it owns, ending at *Claude model ID format for opus*; and only source, kind, date, author, passage and score survive. A search whose every hit is weak keeps its best rather than returning an unreadable empty list. **Measured: 10 423 → 1 038 tokens** across a turn's three searches, with the violin keeping its five best notes and its five conversations.

**Facts of a life** (`services/lifeFacts.ts`, 20 September 2026): a third kind of memory, for what fits neither a brief nor a passage — *"Emilio a onze ans"*, small, stable, true until it changes. Maurice writes one down when he learns it, through the native **`remember_fact`**, but he **proposes** it: the card goes out in the reply and the fact reaches no prompt until the member has kept it. Kept facts sit under the member's own `profile_text`, which stays theirs. Guards: **two judgements per turn**, **200 characters** (longer is a brief in disguise), never the same sentence twice unless it was dismissed. Every proposal is judged once more by the model pinned to the **`life_fact`** ancillary invocation — the calling model changes from thread to thread and this judgement should not — which answers `KEEP` or `DROP` and may reword; it runs in the member's turn, is charged to them, is spared the refusals that cost nothing, and **fails open**. `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/life-facts` (`routes/lifeFacts.ts`), `LifeFactCard.swift` for the two buttons.

**Gaps**: no temporal mirror; flashcards have no backup; the nightly does not re-walk the file sources (the gateway's watcher alone notices a garden file changed while the server was down, and a seeded note reaches the index the same way); the shared pool answers without the books until the re-index reaches them; tracks' deep research is the least tested; the import's job lives in the gateway's memory (`import_status` answers "unknown job" after a restart) and the app reads the whole `.zip` into memory before uploading it. The knowledge note's gap that the nightly cannot prune the shared `_default.db` is contradicted by the server note (swept since the evening of the 19th with `shared: true`); the server note is the later one.

## Applications, plugins, and the case of the mail

A parked product thread (17 September 2026): how a personal application such as mail triage attaches to Maurice without the product reading as a pile of experiments. "Plugin" conflates maturity (experimental) and architecture (extension); mail is an extension and no longer experimental. The generic pattern worth extracting: a closed vocabulary as the containment boundary, a proposal before any write, deterministic rules before the model, an audit log, failures that announce themselves.
